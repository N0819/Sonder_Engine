Layout Lab

Each building was described to the room designer's model, which wrote only relations: what joins what and on which side, what stands against which wall. The solver sizes every room from what it holds and grows each storey room by room, standing on the storey below. Each stair stands against a wall of the room at both ends, at the same cells on both storeys. The building stands inside its grounds. One square is a pace (0.75 m).
