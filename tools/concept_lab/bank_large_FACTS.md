# Canonical facts sheet -- large concept bank (t001-t240)

The bible's outline governs plot. Where it is silent, the version most memories
already carried (or the one that reads most naturally) was chosen. Every edited
memory in `week*.json` agrees with this sheet.

## Calendar

- Story day N = **N October** for N = 1..31; day 32 = 1 November ... day 56 = 25 November.
  (Chosen because weeks 1, 3, 5, 6 and 7 all quote dates equal to the day number --
  "the ninth", "the tenth", "the fifteenth", "17th", "30th" -- and week 7's "13th/14th"
  for days 43/44 fits only a 31-day first month. An October start also keeps Christmas
  out of week 8, which never mentions it.) The year is never named.
- **Day 1 is a Monday.** Mondays: 1, 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, 50. Sundays: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56.
- Saturday is market day in the square. Schools sit Monday to Saturday.
- Snow: first on the ridge day 6; first proper snow in town day 12; repeated falls after.
  The weather thaws around day 48.
- Useful dates: collapse day 10 (Wed 10 Oct); tunnel breaks into the old workings
  day 9 (9 Oct); Hale arrives evening of day 14; first fever day 18 (Thu 18 Oct);
  Pavel Hruby dies day 24 (Wed 24 Oct, 4.40 a.m.); Brenner arrives day 24;
  bunkhouse fire day 31 (Wed 31 Oct); well closed day 36 (Mon 5 Nov); last new
  fever case day 38 (Wed 7 Nov); parents' evening and the kiss day 41 (Sat 10 Nov);
  Anton reports gas day 43 (Mon 12 Nov); Ines confronts Voss day 44 (Tue 13 Nov);
  explosion day 46 (Thu 15 Nov, 11.40 a.m.); funeral day 48 (Sat 17 Nov);
  inquiry day 52 (Wed 21 Nov); refusal posted day 54 (Fri 23 Nov, 10.40 mail);
  Lotte and Mira move in day 55 (Sat); the walk to the mine day 56 (Sun 25 Nov).

  raised by a crown a week (day 45) and to four crowns a week (day 51).

  Kept under the blotter, then in the top desk drawer. Ines drafts an acceptance
  day 28 (folded into the casebook, never sent); writes "Dear Sirs" only on day 42;
  writes and posts the refusal day 54 and burns the offer and the old draft.

## The fever

- Cause: the tunnel broke into the lower end of **Gallery Two** of the old silver mine
  on day 9; mine water drains down a flume/gully and soaks in 40 yards above the
  **upper well** (by the spoil heap, above bunkhouse two). Ines drank from it,
  still clear, on day 6.
- Upper well serves bunkhouses **one, two and four** and the upper cookhouse; the lower
  spring serves **three (burned day 31), five and six**. Burned-out men were moved
  into bunkhouse one.
- Running case count (cumulative): d18 2 (Mraz, Horvat, bunkhouse two); d19 4;
  d20 5; d21 6; d22 7; d23 9; d24 Pavel Hruby dies; d26 11; d28 11 (three recovering);
  d29 14 (cases 12-14); d32 19 (16 slept by the upper well, 2 ate at the upper
  cookhouse); d35 21; d36 23 (22 on upper-well water); d38 26 (three after the
  closure, the last Brugger); none after. **Final: 26 cases, one death.**
- **The one death: Pavel Hruby**, 19, navvy from past Brno, died day 24 at 4.40 a.m.
- Well closed **day 36 (5 Nov)** by the mayor's order, 11.20 a.m., Hale present;
  water carts from the town spring, eleven crowns a week.
- Brenner: arrives day 24, "mountain ague"; revises his view day 41; is being
  recalled "when the company's new man comes up, in a fortnight or so", so he is
  still in Kessel Pass for the explosion night (day 46) and the inquiry (day 52),
  where he admits his first report was wrong.
- **Quinine stock:** day 4 crate brings two tins of 25 powders; day 5 stock 53
  powders (locked cupboard); day 18 38 powders + a bottle of tincture; Ines's
  own order to the valley wholesaler refused (postcard, day 23); day 20 Grete's
  tin of 50 on account; day 22 26 left; day 23 an ounce of sulphate + Dover's
  powders from Grete; day 28 about three days' worth; day 30 3 oz from Grete;
  day 35 about 40 grains in the jar; day 37 crate (2 oz + 100 powders); day 38
  41 powders in the ward box; day 40 33, twenty taken back to the clinic.

## The gas thread

- Anton Reis: shot-firer, 31, north gang, mother in Linz. Burned **right palm**
  (a fuse line across the base), first seen day 2 at night, nameless; returns day 8
  infected and gives his name. Palm healed shiny pink by day 14-16.
- Readings: the official log reads **0.4** every shift (Stoll fills it before the
  shift). Ines wrote "readings same every shift - 0.4" in the casebook, scratched it out
  day 14 and rewrote it in ship's shorthand on the back flyleaf.
- **Two books:** the **black company book** (company stamp; kept in the lamp room;
  shown to Hale and to Ines in Voss's hut day 44). The **true book, green cloth
  cover**, hidden **under Stoll's straw mattress in the foreman's hut behind
  bunkhouse two, wrapped in oilcloth** (Anton, day 38; found by Hale with Ines
  as witness day 48, 1.40 p.m.). True readings: 1.5, 2, 2.5 per cent in the last
  week; Tuesday 13 Nov "Face -- 2 1/2 %, men complaining, K.V. says carry on."
  It shows they should have stopped twice in October.
- The safety lamp: No. 27, gauze seam opened and re-crimped with pliers, given by
  Anton at 10.50 p.m. day 17. Kept **locked in the dispensary press** (key on the
  string round Ines's neck). Taken to Voss's hut day 44; wrapped in a tea towel for
  the inquiry.
- Explosion day 46, 11.40 a.m.: **Stoll killed**; burned: **Anton** (both forearms,
  left side of face, left eye), **Karl Ebner**, **Fritzi Brook** (hands; mother in
  Silesia), **Lorenz** (both legs). Dressings: picric and oiled silk. Brenner helps
  from 2 a.m. Hale arrives with Ott just after 7 a.m. day 47.
- Funeral day 48 at 10 at St. Veit's; Stoll's sister from Innsbruck; Voss attends.
- **Inquiry:** Wednesday **day 52**, from 9 a.m., in the **station waiting room**,
  three men from the railway board, chairman Gerlach. Ines called at 10.10. Hale
  presents both books after the midday break. Finding at 5.30: Voss dismissed and
  handed to the district police (constables from Grauberg). Hale leaves day 56 on
  the noon train; Voss to be tried in the city in spring.
- Hale: first seen evening day 14 in a grey coat, introduces himself day 17,
  **lodges at the Hart** throughout.

## Accidents and patients

- Heading collapse day 10, four men: **Luka Maric** (femur; nursed in Ines's back room,
  gone to the valley hospital day 15), **Karel Horn** (crushed left foot), **Jiri Benes**
  (ribs 5-7 and clavicle), **Tomas Kral** (depressed skull fracture, trephined).
- Bunkhouse fire day 31, bunkhouse three, cracked stove flue: **Wenzel Haas**
  (forearms, hands) and **Dimitri Lukas** (back, thighs). Up and walking by day 38.
- Lamp fire in the dispensary day 12, just after nine p.m.; Lotte smothers it.
- Mila Brandt, 9, weak chest; worst night day 27-28; clear by day 43 and still clear.
- Births: Frau Weber's fourth (day 2, a girl); the Egger farm (day 39, a girl).
- Name hygiene: Frau Pichler keeps the post office; old Herr Pichler (gout) lives on
  Tanner Street; the mastitis patient of day 31 is Frau Moser; the carter's wife
  (heartburn) is Frau Wendt; old Frau Lechner has the cough and the bunions.
  Haas the cobbler (Kirchgasse) repairs Ines's left boot days 27-32.

## Family

- Lotte arrives day 4 on the 11.20 with Mira and two trunks; lodges at the Hart.
- Mira, 8 ("nine in March"); first words to Ines day 19 about a **sparrow** with a
  broken wing (bound with lint; lives in a shoebox at the clinic days 20-22, then
  by the school stove from day 23).
- **Friedrich's letter** arrives by post at the Hart around day 13; Ines sees Lotte
  hide it in her sewing basket that afternoon. It demands she return by **1 December**
  and threatens to come for Mira. Lotte tells all day 33 (drinking, beatings since
  Easter two years ago, a rib broken in March, debts of nearly 2,300 crowns);
  they burn the letter that night.
- Bruises on Lotte's forearm seen day 19; yellowing day 22; gone by day 34.
- **The brooch:** the will (read in a solicitor's office in Graz nine years ago;
  Lotte 22, Ines 29) gave it to **Lotte**. Lotte kept it sewn into her coat lining so
  Friedrich could not sell it. Ines has not seen it in nine years until day 42; one
  garnet missing, clasp will not shut. Lotte wears it, later boxes it; Ines mends the
  clasp day 55 and it goes on the mantel.
- Rooms: the two back rooms upstairs at the clinic (one over the dispensary), offered
  day 4, 34, 42, 51; Lotte and Mira move in day 55. Mira calls her "Aunt Ines" day 55.

## Petr and the old clinic

- Petr Anko, 11, died six years ago on the table in the old clinic on Mill Lane,
  **under chloroform** during a bowel (appendix) operation. Eight days earlier Ines
  had noted a soft systolic murmur, "query -- recheck", and never rechecked.
- Old clinic: shutters nailed, **brass plate taken down, four screw holes left in
  the stone**; the operating room is the second window. Ines counts forty-two steps
  to the corner. She goes in day 49 and sits fifty minutes.
- Marta Anko: turns away day 6 (market), day 20 (market), day 35 (grave); lets Ines
  in day 53 (end house, Tanner's Row); nods at her day 56. Petr's birthday 9 March.

## Other anchors

- Mine map: 1884 survey, left to the parish by the last mine captain; borrowed day 17,
  returned day 39 (before Advent). Gallery Four hatched: "Fall of 14 March 1891.
  Eleven men." Ines traces on tracing paper, which a pencil line once pressed through.
- Teodor Lind arrives day 5; quarrel day 24, make up day 26; draws the camp plan day
  34-35; kiss day 41; walk to the mine day 56.
- Casebook: many volumes, one per year; for the inquiry Ines takes this year's and
  the first.
