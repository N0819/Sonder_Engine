## Why this document remains

This record explains the thesis that started the calibration. The artifact
continued through many product-owner review cycles after the original static
brief: typography changed to Geist Sans + Geist Mono + Newsreader; CRT material
grain and animated noise were removed; rounded 4 px bevels replaced chamfers;
theme controls became live; character portraits became scalable; toolbars
became collapsible/resizable; and modules gained shelves, tabs, floating,
Widget Shelf recovery, clear drag previews, and height-derived capacity.

Those evolved decisions are integrated directly into Design Bible 2.0. Any
scope limitation or provisional rule from the earlier brief is historical and
must not be used to contradict the current Bible or artifact.

## Original thesis retained

> Sonder is a quiet fiction instrument made from black digital material,
> suspended over a living atmosphere.

The calibration sought one coherent visual object combining:

- compact editorial navigation and atmospheric restraint;
- a living full-screen canvas;
- high-density modern typography;
- literary story presentation;
- translucent configurable digital material; and
- a bounded creative-tool docking model.

The key acceptance principle also remains:

> A result that merely checks off glass, small type, accents, and modular panels
> but reads as a generic dashboard is not conforming.

## Provenance boundary

Prime Intellect, EVE/Photon, PS2-era material, ChungusHub, Photoshop, and VS
Code were discussed as influences. No external source, markup, CSS, assets,
branding, or proprietary font files are part of the artifact. Design Bible 2.0
records which ideas survived and which were rejected.
