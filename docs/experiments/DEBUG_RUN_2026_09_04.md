## F1. A thought-only reply kills the Room's grant, four attempts running — FIXED 2026-09-05
- Run: tools/room_bench.py --chat 114 (copy), first attempt 17:39.
- The Story Planner's main call (agents/story_planner.py:612, PLANNER_MAX_TOKENS=20000) raised
  `ReasoningBudgetExhausted: google/gemini-3.8-flash returned reasoning but no answer (6159 chars of trace, content empty)`
  after RetryConfig.max_retries=3 (four attempts), the last three with `reasoning: {enabled: false}` on the request
  (providers._apply_reasoning_effort). 6159 chars is ~1.5k tokens: the budget was NOT exhausted; the model simply
  returned an empty answer with a trace, and disabling reasoning did not change that. A tiny probe on the same role
  answered twice in a row; the rerun of the same grant got past the point. So: intermittent, prompt-size dependent,
  and the retry's one lever (effort off) is not the lever.
- Class: a retry loop that varies one setting cannot recover from a failure that setting does not cause. The role
  has a backup-candidate mechanism (`candidates[candidate_offset]`) that this path never reaches.
- Effect: the player's grant is lost with an error in the panel; nothing lands (safe), but the Room looks broken.
- Proposal (not built): after N reasoning-only replies, fall to the role's next candidate (the `repair` role's
  GLM on provider 6 exists in the owner's agent_models) for the remaining attempts, and log the finish_reason.
- **Built 2026-09-05**, together with its untyped sibling (the road run's two dead Writers' Room calls, which came
  back as "response message carried no content" carrying `reasoning_details`). One rule covers both: `_reasoning_text`
  reads the trace off whatever key SAYS it is reasoning, so a reply that carries only reasoning and no content is
  this typed failure however the seam spelled the field; and after `REASONING_ONLY_BEFORE_CANDIDATE` (2) of them the
  sync retry loop advances `candidate_offset` and spends its remaining attempts on the role's next model, starting
  again from the caller's own effort.
