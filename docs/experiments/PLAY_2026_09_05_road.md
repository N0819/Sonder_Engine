## 3. Findings

### PD1. Two bodies standing together at one anchor are placed at opposite ends of it
- **Stage of origin:** `world/spatial_fov.body_cell` (the `at` branch runs
  before the `near` branch).
- **Live case.** Turn 18/19/20, the hilltop and the gate. Both stations read
  `{"at": "scrub_edge", "near": ["Corin Ashe"]}` and
  `{"at": "scrub_edge", "near": ["Sable"]}` — each names the other as the body
  it is standing with. Derived cells: Corin `(4, 3)`, Sable `(10, 3)` — **six
  paces apart on a `run` anchor spanning a 15-pace wall**, while the composed
  view of the same beat says "Sableはあなたの右側、手の届く距離にいる" (within
  arm's reach). Same shape at the birch stand (turn 7: `(2, 8)` and `(2, 11)`)
  and the gatehouse (turn 20: `(2, 0)` and `(2, 3)`).
- Consequence: the player's own declared line reached nobody on turn 18 and
  reached Sable as a three-word mutter on turn 20 (PD2/PD3 carry the rest).
- **Severity:** story-breaking.
- **Recurs:** no registered id; it is the mechanism under F58's "stationed at
  an anchor, standing three cells from the thing named".
- **Fix.** `body_cell`: `near` is the record that says these two are TOGETHER,
  and it must outrank the anchor's own spread. Resolve a body whose station
  names another body already placed in this room beside that body's cell —
  clamped to the anchor's cells when both share an `at` — and let the
  hash-seeded spread along the anchor apply only to a body with no such
  companion. Test: two bodies at one `run` anchor, each `near` the other, in a
  15x20 room ⇒ adjacent cells, and a normal voice between them grades `full`.

### PD2. Outdoors, ordinary speech is `full` only inside about three paces
- **Stage of origin:** `world/spatial_sound_field` constants
  (`SPEECH_POWER["normal"] = 12`, `AMBIENT["open"] = 0.2` plus weather,
  `FULL_SNR = 2.0`, decay `P/(1+d²)`).
- **Live case, probed on the committed scene of turn 20** — an EMPTY gate room,
  no sound entity in it, light rain: `gain=0.0648 noise=0.500`, so a normal
  voice at three cells yields `signal=0.78` against the `1.00` that `full`
  needs. Grades: `{whisper: none, normal: fragment, loud: full, shout: full}`.
  At six cells (turn 18) a normal voice is `none`. Solving the same
  inequality: **the full-speech radius outdoors in light rain is d ≤ 3.3
  paces.** In the clearing with a campfire (turn 15) the radius closes to
  under two: `Hob → Corin normal: gain=0.0648 noise=2.822 signal=0.78 → none`,
  which is why the burner's answer to a direct question (§ PD8's other half)
  reached nobody.
- **Severity:** story-breaking — a two-hander walking a road cannot converse.
- **Fix:** owner decision, and the question is which constant is wrong rather
  than whether one is. The measurement says either `AMBIENT["open"]` is a noise
  floor an unremarkable outdoor scene should not carry, or `SPEECH_POWER`'s
  ladder is calibrated for a room. State the rule the constants must satisfy —
  *two people walking together on an open road converse in full; the road
  takes their voices at the distance you would have to raise your voice in
  life* — and pick the constant that makes it true. Test: an empty `open` room,
  fair weather, two bodies four paces apart, normal volume ⇒ `full`.

### PD3. A fragment from a speaker the observer can see is delivered anonymously, and the narrator invents a source for it
- **Stage of origin:** the composer's fragment rendering (`agents/composer.py`);
  the invention is the narrator's, and it is licensed by what it was handed.
- **Live case, turn 20.** Sable's `perception_act` view: `"A muffled voice:
  ...milestone... ferryman... business..."` — that is Corin, her employer,
  three paces in front of her, in daylight, whom the same view describes in
  full. Her appraisal then reads `"A muffled voice from the gatehouse mentions
  'milestone... ferryman... business...'"`, and her committed memory row is
  `"I heard a muffled voice: ...milestone... ferryman... business..."`. The
  narrator wrote: *"Above the roadway, a muffled voice drifts down through the
  timbers of the watch platform."* Same shape at turn 12 (her own line rendered
  as coming "from inside" the turf hut) and turn 19.
- **Why this is the worst one.** A percept whose SOURCE is stripped does not
  stay stripped: a mind fills it, the narrator fills it, and the Writers' Room
  recap then repeated the invention back to me as fact ("Sable scouted the turf
  hut and heard movement inside"). Nothing crossed a channel it should not
  have, so it is not a firewall breach — but the durable record now says an
  unseen third party at the gatehouse knows about a ferryman and a debt, and no
  stage authored that.
- **Severity:** story-breaking, and it corrupts memory.
- **Recurs:** F61's family (two graders for one utterance), one field over.
- **Fix, two halves.** (a) `composer`: a partially-heard line from a speaker
  the observer can SEE and recognises keeps its attribution — *"Sable says
  something you do not catch"*, never "a muffled voice"; anonymity is for a
  speaker the observer cannot place. (b) `agents/narration.py` prompt: an
  unattributed sound is rendered as unattributed; the narrator may not supply
  a place or a body for a voice the view did not site. Test: a fragment from a
  visible known speaker renders with the speaker's name; the episode written
  from it names the speaker too.

### PD5. A planned edge with no `barrier` is normalized to `wall`, sealing the road the plan drew — FIXED 2026-09-05
- **Stage of origin:** `world/spatial_barriers.normalize_barrier` (its last
  line) reading an edge the Room's plan wrote without the optional field.
- **Live case.** `plan_rooms` marks `barrier?` optional, and the Room omitted
  it on every edge of its five-room road. At turn 3 the ford landed
  `adjacent: [{to: the_road_on_toward_the_ford, bearing: south}]` with no
  barrier and normalization made it `"barrier": "wall"`; the wood road's three
  edges (ford, clearing, hilltop) the same at turn 7. I had to open four edges
  by hand through `PATCH /rooms/{id} {exits}` for the story to be walkable —
  the world's own plan had built the road and then walled it.
- **Severity:** story-breaking (it stops the walk), invisible (no warning).
- **Recurs:** the mirror of F38 — the same class, on absence rather than
  one-sidedness. The function's own docstring records that "return `wall`" for
  an unread word cost 14.6% of live barriers; an ABSENT word is the same
  failure with an emptier input.
- **Fix.** An edge that declares no barrier declares an opening, not a wall:
  `normalize_barrier(None)` should answer `open` (the value `room_create`
  already defaults to, `web/world_routes.py:1719`), and only a non-empty word
  the table cannot read should fall to `wall`, still collecting into
  `unresolved`. Test: a `plan_rooms` op with a bare `{"to": X}` edge publishes
  a passable edge, and `normalize_barrier("")` is `open` while
  `normalize_barrier("bulkhead")` stays `wall` and is reported.

### PD7. The clock's cumulative elapsed drives a weather re-roll on every beat, and it overwrites the Director's declared sky — FIXED 2026-09-05
- **Stage of origin:** `world/weather.advance_weather` +
  `persist/commit_scene_state` (the `elif sc.get("weather")` branch).
- **Live case.** `step = elapsed_seconds // DRIFT_SECONDS` is CUMULATIVE, and
  is used only as a roll salt; the transition itself walks `_SKY_NEXT` from
  whatever sky the scene currently has. So once the clock passes one hour the
  sky takes a fresh hop on every commit. Measured across four consecutive
  beats inside one drift window (step 8): turn 10 `fair → overcast/drizzle/
  light`, turn 11 `overcast → storm/rain/heavy/gale`, turn 12 the Director
  DECLARED `overcast/drizzle/light/breeze` (which wins, correctly), turn 13
  `overcast → storm/rain/heavy/gale` again — **light drizzle and a breeze to a
  gale in fifteen seconds of story time, and the Director's declaration
  reverted the beat after it landed.** The docstring promises the opposite:
  "Returns the input unchanged inside one drift window, so an ordinary
  conversational beat does not move the weather at all."
- Downstream: that gale is why the clearing's noise floor was 2.8 at turn 15
  (PD2), so an invented storm silenced a conversation.
- **Severity:** wrong-but-recoverable, but it is loud in the prose and it
  quietly moves the sound field.
- **Fix.** Record the step the weather was last drifted at on the weather
  record itself and drift only when the step ADVANCES; a Director declaration
  stamps the current step so it stands for the rest of the window. That is the
  invariant the docstring already claims. Test: five commits inside one
  drift window return one weather; a declaration on the third survives the
  fourth and the fifth; the sixth, in the next window, drifts from the
  declaration.

## 5. The Writers' Room as co-author

**What it did.** Recorded my grant verbatim as a mandate with capabilities it
inferred (`plan_rooms`, `plan_entity`, `create_people`) and a scope in my own
words; drafted, validated and published a five-room plan with adjacency and
frontiers in 52s; filed a `director_note` and a `plan_entity` under a second
mandate it minted from the sentence "leave the Director a note"; read
`inspect_minds` and gave me an accurate account of Sable's drive, strain,
intentions and beliefs that matched her card and her ledger (**F8's fix
holds** — the authored tier was there); refused, correctly and with the right
reason, to write a belief into a mind.

**What it could not do.**
1. **Geometry.** No `extent`, `shape`, `parts`, `light`, `exposure` or anchors
   in `plan_rooms` (**F47**). It worked round this by writing measurements
   into `purpose` prose, and — see PD9 — that actually landed. But it cannot
   author a light source, a sound source, or an anchor with a height, which
   are the three things this scenario needed most.
2. **Regions.** I asked for the wood as its own sheltered region; every room
   went into one region. Asked to fix it, it answered exactly as an honest
   collaborator should: *"Speaking plainly: I have no tool to create, split, or
   alter regions ... Because there is no operation or command in my mandate to
   reassign or separate regions directly, I cannot force the wood into its own
   isolated region, and I will not pretend it is done."* That is the right
   answer and the gap is real: `PATCH /api/chats/{cid}/regions/{id}` exists
   for the host and there is no `plan_rooms.region` and no region tool.
3. **Barriers.** It omitted `barrier` on every planned edge, which is legal
   (`barrier?`) and sealed the road (PD5).

**Where it overstepped.** Not in authority — it never authored a mind, never
touched the player's conduct, never invented a world fact outside a package.
It overstepped in **narration of causes**. Its recap asserted "Corin crossed
behind her after offering comfrey salve for her cheek scar, **which she
declined**" and "Sable scouted the turf hut and **heard movement inside**".
Neither is in the record: the ledger has a contact (her hand closing on his
around the tin) and an inventory transfer, and the "movement inside" is PD3's
invented source read back as fact. Challenged, it corrected the second
honestly ("that was a misstatement; the objective world record confirms the
hut has been entirely silent") and defended the first by reading intent out of
a physical record ("her hand remained stiff and closed rather than taking
it" — the record says no such thing). **The class: the recap reads the
ledgers, which record positions, contacts and transfers, and renders them as
motives.** A recap should say what the ledger says and mark an inference as
one.

**What I would improve, in order.**
1. Give `plan_rooms` the four fields the world already owns — `extent`,
   `shape`, `exposure`, `region` — and let `plan_entity` carry the source
   fields (`light_source`, `light_height`, `steadiness`, `sound_source`). Every
   one is a closed set the engine defines; none of them is a word list about
   English. This is the single change that would have removed four of my
   twelve findings.
2. Default a planned edge's barrier to `open` (PD5) — a plan that draws a road
   should not build a wall.
3. Make the recap cite. Every clause in it should be traceable to a ledger row
   or be marked as the Room's reading.
4. **FIXED 2026-09-05.** Two of my five Room calls died on `LLMError:
   response message carried no content` after ~120s and 7-14 tool calls, with
   a `reasoning_details` block
   and `content: None`. That is **F1's sibling and it is not covered by F1's
   retry**: `_is_f1` matches `ReasoningBudgetExhausted` / "returned reasoning
   but no answer", and this failure says "response message carried no
   content" — `llm/providers.py:2861`, the branch taken when there is neither
   content nor a `reasoning` STRING (the trace came back under
   `reasoning_details` instead). Both attempts were lost whole; the third,
   with a shorter brief and "do not inspect anything first", succeeded in 52s.
   Fix: read `reasoning_details` as reasoning at that branch so the typed
   exception is raised and the existing retry (and F1's proposed
   candidate-fallback) covers it.
   Built as ONE rule rather than two special cases: `_reasoning_text` reads the
   trace off whatever key SAYS it is reasoning (`_capture_reasoning` shares it
   now), so a reply that carries only reasoning and no content raises F1's
   typed failure whichever field the trace arrived in -- and after
   `REASONING_ONLY_BEFORE_CANDIDATE` (2) of them the sync retry loop advances
   `candidate_offset` and finishes its attempts on the role's next model.


## 6. Prompt and payload proposals, per stage

**`director_interpret` (payload 5.7-17.0 KB, 21.4s mean).** It is sent the
whole `scene` (up to 10.3 KB) and `director_recent_messages` (3.7 KB) to
decide flow, speech, movement and claims. `scene` is the largest key in the
payload and interpret does not place anything — the rooms in reach and the
positions would do. **Proposal:** send interpret the room window it already
computes for the hands, not the scene. Second: the interpret's own repair call
(`sys=3348`, 5 of 20 beats) is a good seam and its `dropped_declarations` were
right every time — keep it, but its output re-raised a `generation_request`
for a figure the plan already held (PD8), so the repair should see
`present_figures` too.

**The five specialists.** `player_declaration` (up to 3.1 KB) is sent VERBATIM
to `body`, `contact`, `objects`, `social` and `spatial` on every beat — five
copies of the same paragraph, and the same again as `resolved_event` at
resolve. `director_spatial` additionally carries `rooms` at 11.0 KB, which is
every room of the scene with every anchor, on a beat where one body walked one
edge. **Proposal:** the spatial hand needs the origin room, the destination
room and their neighbours; the other four hands need no `rooms` map at all
(they were sent one — 0.8-1.0 KB of names — and none of them writes a room).
Measured cost of the current shape: the spatial hand was the slowest sub-call
of the run (23.6s at turn 5, 24.4s at turn 11) and its answer was usually two
fields.

**`director_resolve` (payload 14.5-26.5 KB, 34.4s mean, the run's cost
centre).** It carries `scene` AND `prior_scene` (10.3 + 8.7 KB) plus
`previous_state_diff` (3.3 KB) — three overlapping representations of the same
world. It also ran a full second call on 4 of 20 beats for the self-repair,
each ~20 KB, i.e. ~40 KB of payload to fix one sentence. **Proposal:** the
repair call should be given the offending sentences and the constraint, not
the whole beat again (it already gets `correction_notes`; the payload beside
it is what costs). And the interiority guard that triggered two of those four
repairs fired on the word **"know"** inside a line of DIALOGUE — *"Leave the
pack strapped till we know who's inside"*, *"Not sitting till I know what's
under that turf"* — which is a quoted character's own speech, not the
narration asserting the player's interior. That is a free-prose guard failing
in the direction its missing distinction points (CLAUDE.md's rule); it should
not read inside quotation marks.

**`character_mid` (payload 13-55 KB, the largest of any role).** `memory` grew
to 25.6 KB and `self` to 22.3 KB by turn 20, and `memory.recalled_old_memories`
delivers whole composed VIEWS as the memory's `details` — a full room
description, its anchors, the other body's clothing, verbatim, per memory.
**Proposal:** a memory's `details` should be what the mind kept, not the view
it was minted from; the view is already re-derivable and is 10x the size of
the fact. Also: `character Sable: no delivered present observation was cited`
fired on **17 of 20 beats** — the claims floor is warning on nearly every beat
of a two-hander, which means it is measuring something the payload's shape
makes hard rather than a fault of the mind. Worth deciding whether the
observation ids the payload hands it are the ones the model is asked to cite.
*(Answered 2026-09-05: it was measuring itself. The floor read a wire lane
retired from the ask while Sable's citations sat in
`appraisal.present_evidence`. Closed — flat run PE20.)*

**`narrator` (payload 1.9-21.3 KB, 10.0s mean).** `past_narration` reached
13.1 KB by turn 20 and is the largest key. The quote guards fired 9 times over
the run on **doubled quotation marks** (`""line""`) — **F54/F29 recur, on 6 of
20 beats** — and every one of those warnings was false. Until the guards
normalise repeated quote runs before matching, they cost a real signal: on
turn 1 the "Narrator invented quoted dialogue" warning fired three times on
lines that were delivered verbatim. **Proposal:** normalise runs of quote
characters before every quote comparison; the guard is right, its matcher is
literal.

**`perception_*` (0.3-0.8s, deterministic, no payload).** Nothing to propose
about size. The one shape change I would make is PD3's: the composed view's
dialogue percept should carry the speaker where the observer has one, because
every consumer downstream (the mind, the narrator, the memory writer) fills
that gap with an invention if it is left empty.

**`background_react`.** Payload 0.85-3.1 KB, 1.4s per call, two calls when it
fires — the cheapest stage that produces speech, and its line was dropped
without a word (PD8). Its `institutional_context` (1.1 KB) is the charter's,
for a burner the charter had just invented a household for.
