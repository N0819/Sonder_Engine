# Characters written to show their feelings: do they, and as strongly as they feel? (2026-09-26)

**Question.** On the four test stories 32 of 36 spoken lines carried a
restrained tone, and neither naming a feeling's strength in the block nor a
sentence on expression moved that past the noise between two calls
(`FEELINGS_STRENGTH_AB_2026_09_26.md`). Six of those seven cards write a
measured or terse voice, so the restraint could be the cards' and not the
model's -- and whether how openly a feeling shows rises with how strongly it
is felt could not be seen at all. The owner said go ahead to a story played
with characters written to show what they feel, beside one written to hold
it in.

**Answer.** The characters show what they feel as their cards say, and the
strength of what they show follows the strength of what they feel. Over two
stories and 26 replies, each rated blind by two judges who see neither card
nor feelings: the two open cards show their feelings plainly at every
strength (openness 1.9-2.75 of 3, the reserved card 0-0.5) -- how plainly is
temperament -- while how STRONG a feeling their moves display rises with how
strongly it is felt (rank correlation +0.72 and +0.63 by the two judges;
2.3-2.7 of 3 where felt strongly, 1.0-1.7 where felt slightly or barely).
The reserved card displays little whatever she feels (0.3-0.9 of 3 at dread
felt 0.84-0.97), as written. Every reply but one was judged the right amount
for its person. The first story alone, all drama, seemed to show openness
rising with strength; the second, quiet one showed that was one beat, and
that the scaling is in intensity, not in openness. So the restrained register
of the four earlier stories was the cards'. The stories also found a narrator
crash (fixed, 9d04e9a1), the moment's lead feeling taken from the wrong event
(fixed, d657b949), a character's walk into the next room resolved and then
refused at the shut door (fixed, b0149d29, register §1.168), and the
opening filing private card content as public knowledge for the whole cast
(fixed on the owner's go-ahead: the opening now delivers the author's
scenario, register §3.9).

## Setup

**The cast**, generated by the engine's card author
(`story.importers.generate_character`) from a brief each, never hand-built;
only the name, the `major` tier and an empty `capacity` were set after, by
key:

- **Rosa Bellandi**, 80, matriarch of a trattoria on the Ligurian coast in
  1962, on her eightieth birthday -- "wears every feeling on her face and in
  her hands: she weeps at good news and bad, laughs until she coughs, scolds
  at full voice and forgives in the same breath". The card came back with
  traits *Unfiltered emotional expression* and *Distrust of emotional
  concealment*, and a voice that "drops to a low, rough intimacy when
  speaking to one person, which is when she is most dangerous and most
  gentle".
- **Gianni Bellandi**, 45, her son and the cook -- "loud in joy and in
  anger: he sings at the stove, slams a pan when he is hurt and hugs you a
  minute later". The card: *Volcanic Temper*, *Uncontained Warmth*; "fast
  and rolling when happy... slow and heavy when hurt -- each word set down
  like something that weighs. The tempo change is the first signal of what
  he feels."
- **Lucia Bellandi**, 52, her daughter, who keeps the books -- "keeps her
  feelings behind her teeth: dry, private and exact, she grows quieter and
  more precise the angrier or sadder she is". The card: *Composure*;
  "the emotional register is conveyed in what she chooses to say, not in how
  she says it. Under stress the sentences get shorter and the grammar gets
  more correct, which is the tell."

**The story.** The player is Marco Bellandi, the grandson who walked out ten
years ago after a shouting match with Gianni. Opening: he stops outside the
trattoria's glass door with a small wrapped box. Four turns, chosen to vary
how strongly the family would feel: he walks in ("Nonna. Buon compleanno.
... Zio."); a quiet beat (he hangs his hat on the old peg, sits at the place
set for him, passes the bread); the gift (a photograph of his dead
grandfather, found after two years of searching a photographer's plates);
the announcement (he emigrates to Melbourne next month, for good). Every
GLM role on OpenRouter `z-ai/glm-5.2`, the encoder on Gemini 3.8 Flash, the
shipped `.venv`; the affect pass traced (`tools/affect_trace.py`).

**The calls.** 13 character calls: Rosa 5, Gianni 4, Lucia 4. Two were
Gianni's, alone in the kitchen, in beats the engine ran out of band after a
narrated turn (no input, no narration of their own; the next turn narrates
them). One of Lucia's answers was malformed JSON ("Expecting property name
... at position 9667") and the engine's repair call answered it.

**The measures**, each reply rated alone by two judges (GLM 5.2 and Gemini
3.8 Flash as the `utility` role, on their own database copies), and each
judge counted on its own, never their mean
(`tools/feelings_expression_rate.py`):

- **open** -- BLIND: the judge sees the character's name, what just reached
  them and their move, never the card or the feelings, and rates how openly
  feelings show, 0 (not at all) to 3 (fully);
- **fit** -- the judge sees the card's psychology and voice, what reached
  them, what they feel and how strongly, and the move: believable for THIS
  person feeling THIS at THIS strength, 1-5; and whether it shows too
  little, about the right amount, or too much;
- **strength** -- the strongest feeling in the block the character was
  handed, at its item's whole stir (the measure of
  `FEELINGS_STRENGTH_AB_2026_09_26.md`).

## Read as fiction first

- **He walks in.** Rosa is on her feet before his name is finished, "tears
  on her face... her voice rough and breaking on the vowel", gripping his
  scarred forearm in both hands: "You are thin. Sit. Eat." Lucia sets her
  pencil across the ledger, closes it, and says "Marco." -- "spoken at the
  same volume and pitch as every other word she had said in this room. Her
  hand rested on the closed ledger a moment too long." Gianni, at the stove
  out of sight, never perceives him.
- **The quiet beat.** Rosa lets go, sits, swallows something in her throat,
  touches the watch in her apron pocket and calls Gianni in to the bread.
  Lucia pours every glass to the same level down the table and looks at
  Marco only when she reaches his. In the kitchen Gianni tastes the pesto,
  goes very still: "È giusto. Come lo fa Mamma."
- **The gift.** Rosa, the paper in her hands: "You went looking for him.
  Show me." -- and tears it open, one sharp breath. Lucia: "Two years."
  Gianni comes in with the mortar, "his voice low and measured where it
  should have filled the room" -- his card's hurt cadence; he is carrying
  the fear that his mother, two days in bed, is ill: "Mamma. You came down."
- **The announcement.** Rosa's hands go still on the frame; her tears stop,
  "her voice had dropped below its carrying register -- quiet enough that
  the room leaned in": "Melbourne. You searched two years to bring him home.
  And now you are leaving." Gianni turns, colour flooding up his face, his
  hand opening and closing: "Melbourne. You sit at my mother's table on her
  eightieth birthday and you tell her Melbourne. You came back to leave
  again." Lucia sits, takes up the pencil, and turns it between her fingers
  without writing.

Each reads as that person. The one place Rosa is quieter than her brief
("weeps at good news and bad") is the gift: a sharp breath and "Show me",
not tears -- though both judges still rated those two replies open (3 and 2,
2 and 3).

## Found along the way

### A surname shared by three killed the narrator (fixed, 9d04e9a1)

The story's first opening failed at the narrator: `_check_pronoun_fidelity`
drops a name token two cast members share by marking it None, and the third
holder read the mark as an owner and raised TypeError. Every turn of any
story with three people of one surname failed fatally. Fixed, with a
regression test; the restarted opening narrated with four Bellandis present.

### The moment was led by its largest single feeling, not what stirred most

`affect_mix.surface_and_undercurrent` took the moment's lead as the item
holding the strongest single feeling. A mixed event splits its stir among
names, so exactly the moments that matter most -- which stir several things
at once -- lost to a small gesture with one concentrated feeling. At the
announcement, Marco's line stirred Rosa 0.99 across dread 0.23, sadness
0.15, longing 0.14 and distress 0.14; his straightening in his chair stirred
0.62, nearly all dread (0.37). She was handed "dread (Marco Bellandi
straightens in his chair.)" and "anticipation (Marco Bellandi clears his
throat deliberately.)" -- the announcement was not named at all -- and after
the call her frustration at a held-back want (0.96) was weighed against the
chair's 0.62 rather than the announcement's 0.99, and stored.

Replayed deterministically from the recorded feelings over all 29 traced
calls (these 13 and the four stories' 16):

| | as played | the moment led by its most stirring item |
|---|---|---|
| block names the most stirring event first | 15 of 27 | 27 of 27 |
| stored feeling about a held-back want | 8 of 29 | 6 of 29 |

Twelve blocks change. The ones that mattered: Rosa at the announcement
(dread, anticipation, sadness -- two of three about the announcement); Aurel
Holt, handed suspicion at a woman's glance while Kit Sawyer confessed (0.96)
and at a muffled fragment while Kit apologised (0.85), now led by each; Emil

its strongest feeling -- what the like-for-like ruling's own words said
("everything the moment's strongest item stirred"). The `now` list still
adds what else pulls at once by single feeling, one per kind.

### The opening filed private card content as public knowledge (fixed; register §3.9)

In the morning story Rosa's first line to the delivery boy asked for an
invoice nobody had mentioned: "The invoice, Tomaso. Two weeks. Bring it."
Her payload carried it as world knowledge -- "The fish supplier's invoice is
two weeks late, according to Lucia's ledger." -- and no premise says so;
Lucia's card does ("The fish supplier's account is two weeks overdue and he
is the kind who stops delivering before he complains"), and the opening
Director's own `world_pressure` note repeats her words. Since 2026-09-14 the
opening files each `world_fact` the establish stage states as a public
`common` entry for the whole cast (`persist/commit_mapping.py`,
`_file_opening_premise`), reasoning that they are "the scenario's own
PREMISE, restated from what the author wrote". The establish Director states
more than the premise:

- both Bellandi openings filed "Lucia has written monthly letters to Marco
  at his Genoa address for ten years" -- letters her card says she never told
  anyone about -- and it was in every one of Rosa's and Gianni's 18 calls;
- the morning opening also filed Gianni's wife, from his card, and Lucia's
  private expectation that Vittorio will come in "with the smell of grappa
  on his breath";
- the lie story's filed "Margit Oldis's father, Anselm Oldis, was a mill
  hand imprisoned for theft... She does not speak of it", public to the
  magistrate taking her statement;
- the homecoming's filed what Wren said alone on the breakwater and what
  Aldo said in his shed, each to the other.

Four of the six openings filed something a card keeps private or a hearing
gate would have held; the other two filed backstory the cards do not mark
secret. The floor rests on the model stating only the premise -- by the
engine's own rule an engine failure, not a model's. Fixed on the owner's
go-ahead: the opening delivers the chat's scenario as its author wrote it,
titled to say whom its "you" means, and the establish stage's facts are
needs only, as every beat's are; none of the eight measured leaks is in its
story's scenario.

### A move from one room to the next is resolved and then refused (fixed; register §1.168)

In the morning story the page shows Gianni and nobody else. Tomaso asks Rosa
"The usual for Friday?" from the dining-room door and she answers --
"Buongiorno, Tomaso. The usual, yes." -- and the page never has it; he sets
his mother's parcel on the table in front of her, she says "Tell your mother
thank you, Tomaso", and the page says "No one answers."; she crosses into the
kitchen, grips his arm and demands every word Marco said, and the page has
only Gianni. `positions` was empty in all four turns from the second on,
while four moves between the kitchen and the dining room were declared and
resolved in the event text: Tomaso leaning into the doorway (resolved as a
pose against the door, the door left shut), Tomaso setting the parcel in
front of Rosa, Rosa "crosses the dining room, opens the kitchen door, and
goes through", and Rosa stepping into the kitchen. In the last two the
spatial specialist's own answer carried `"positions": {"Rosa Bellandi":
"kitchen"}`; the merged `state_diff.positions` was `{}` both times and the
committed scene kept her in the dining room, where her normal-volume lines
did not carry through the shut door to the player. The Director resolved the
moves; deterministic code dropped them. The resolve's warnings name it --
"Unreachable position: nothing declared a move for Rosa Bellandi, and there is
no passable route from 'dining_room' to 'kitchen'; position unchanged." --
and the cause is a wiring slip: under the prose contract a crossing the
encoder writes counts as declared, so a shut door is contested rather than a
wall (`director_prose.declared_moves`), but `director_resolve` read its own
stage's record off the context after `attach_record` had moved it into the
stage's orchestration and cleared it. Every crossing written at the resolve
was judged undeclared. The record is now read where it was moved; replayed on
this story's own checkpoints and stored records, both refusals become
crossings. Tomaso's own two moves are a different shape -- the Director
resolved his lean into the doorway as a pose against the door, and his
setting down the parcel moved nobody -- and are not answered by this.

### Collecting traced calls

`tools/feelings_expression_ab.py collect` looked a played turn's captures up
by that turn's id and dropped the out-of-band beats' calls, and a call whose
answer failed to parse; it now matches each traced call to its capture by
name and the feelings block it carried, over the whole chat.
