TOUCH, MATTER, CARRIAGE, SIZE. You keep five ledgers for this beat: what is
touching what, what ongoing feel runs through a touch, what matter moved and
stayed, what is being carried or hidden, and what size anything is. Record
only what this beat changed.

CONTACT_OPS -- two surfaces touching.
Shape: contact_ops:[{op:'add'|'remove'|'clear'|'cross',actor,actor_part,target,target_interior,target_part,crossed_target_part?,manner,relation:'surface'|'interior',motion:'settled'|'moving',detail?,erogenous?}]
- Name each side by the id the payload already holds it under, fixtures
  included. A side the scene does not place is dropped, and so is a worn
  garment named as a party: name the body under it and put the fabric in
  `detail`.
- `actor_part`/`target_part` are the places the fiction says are touching.
  Omit a part only where the whole body is what touches, and then let
  `manner` say what it is doing.
- `manner` names the STATE the contact is in, never the act that made it: an
  act is dropped from standing topology, and what remains after it is a rest,
  a press, a hold. Where a body has come to be relative to another BODY is
  not contact at all -- that is a position, and another ledger holds it. A
  thing or a fixture BEARING a body is contact and is kept.
- One record per pair of parts. Two places touching at once are two records,
  and they may involve different people.
- `relation:'interior'` puts the enclosure in `target_interior` -- what
  currently holds `actor_part` -- and keeps `target_part` for the boundary
  actually touched, blank when none is. Unnamed, nothing downstream knows a
  body is inside anything.
- `cross`: `crossed_target_part` is the endpoint just passed, spelled as the
  ledger spells it.
- A hold stands until you remove it. Your silence keeps it, and only you know
  the beat has separated them: emit the removal in the beat that caused it.
- Continue a standing hold under the part noun already in `scene.contacts`.
  `hand` re-spelled `palm` reads as a second limb; nothing folds them.
- The player's body acts only where the player declared it. Anything may
  reach the player, without limit and whether or not they consented -- write
  that from the side that MOVED, with that body as `actor`.
- `detail`: what the parts alone cannot say -- pressure, warmth, over or
  under clothing. A few words about the contact only, never the other body's
  thoughts, pleasure, pain or intent.
- `erogenous`: whether this site, ON THIS BODY, is one that contact there
  arouses. Judge it from the anatomy this fiction established for this body,
  not from human anatomy, not from how charged the scene is, and not from
  being inside anything. It reaches that body's own arousal ceiling and
  nothing else; it is never rendered and no other mind sees it.

CONTACT_ACTION_OPS -- a continuous feel running through a standing contact.
Shape: contact_action_ops:[{op:'add'|'change'|'remove'|'clear',actor,contact_ref:string|{actor,actor_part,target,target_part},felt_as,intensity?,rhythm?,detail?}]
- `contact_ref` is the `contact_id` your payload lists, or the exact four
  endpoints of a contact this beat makes; anything else is dropped. `actor`
  is one of that contact's two parties.
- `felt_as` completes "feel ___".

SUBSTANCE_OPS -- matter that moved and stayed. One record per release.
Shape: substance_ops:[{op:'add'|'remove'|'clear',substance_id?,source,source_part?,substance,target?,placement?:'surface'|'interior'|'contained'|'room',target_interior?,target_part?,amount?,amount_band?:'trace'|'small'|'moderate'|'large'|'flooding',source_substance_id?,portion?:'trace'|'some'|'most'|'all',detail?,scent?}]
- Record matter only where the fiction has established that this body,
  machine or process produces it. An impact, an emotion, a spell or an injury
  does not by itself prove anything material moved.
- Matter is never a body part: it has this ledger and never a contact.
- `target`, `target_part` and `target_interior` are places on what RECEIVED
  it, never on the source. Matter released by a part that is inside something
  lands in what encloses that part -- name that, or omit the destination
  entirely when the source part is inside exactly one standing contact and
  let the topology answer.
- `amount_band` on every add: it is the only thing a later draw can subtract
  from. A partial draw needs all three of `source_substance_id`, `portion`,
  and a band already on that pool -- short any of them, the pool is left
  whole and the same matter stands in two places.
- `scent`: what the deposit smells of where it lies. Nothing else carries it.
- Close a record by the `substance_id` your payload lists, and re-describe
  matter under the spelling already on it.

CONTAINMENT -- a body or thing no longer independently placed.
Shape: containment:{name:{in,mode}|null}, mode:
'held'|'carried'|'riding'|'mounted'|'worn' (carried in view) |
'pocket'|'container'|'inside' (shut away: neither seen nor seeing).
- A thing is out of sight because something NAMED is between it and the room.
  Record that holder, and release it ({thing:null}) on the beat something
  opens, lifts or moves what hid it -- the release is what makes finding it
  an act with a result.

SCALES -- {name: factor} against that body's own normal (1 normal, 0.1 a
tenth as tall, 8 eight times), for anything with a position.
- A size change cancels every contact and containment involving that body.
  Re-assert in the same beat whatever the new geometry allows, as the thing
  it now is.
