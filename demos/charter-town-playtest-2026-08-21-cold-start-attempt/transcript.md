# Thirty Turns in Aldermere

A model-driven playtest through the production Sonder pipeline.
Only the town fixture and opening establishment were authored.
Every player input and narrator output below is exact.

---

## Turn 1

**Input**

I pause beneath the North Gate and take in the gatehouse, the people on duty, and the town beyond before I speak.

**Output**

The North Gate arches over me, its stonework weathered by centuries of wind and foot traffic. I pause, eyes sweeping across the gatehouse with its heavy timbers and iron fittings, the handful of guards on duty in their worn leather and steel, and the bustling Market Square that opens beyond the open doorway.

Noise spills through from the square, voices and cart wheels and the clatter of goods. The air carries a faint tang of woodsmoke and damp earth. I stand there a moment longer, then speak.
