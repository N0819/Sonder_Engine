# Thirty Turns in Aldermere

A model-driven playtest through the production Sonder pipeline.
Only the town fixture and opening establishment were authored.
Every player input and narrator output below is exact.

---

## Turn 1

**Input**

I pause beneath the North Gate and take in the gatehouse, the people on duty, and the town beyond before I speak.

**Output**

The heavy stones of the North Gate press cool and solid against my shoulder as I pause there. A gatehouse stands just inside, its wooden beams weathered gray. Several figures in tabards hold their posts near the open doorway that leads into Market Square. The town stretches out beyond, all low roofs and morning bustle.

## Turn 2

**Input**

I read the duty board beside the gate, then ask the nearest guard, "Who is in command here?"

**Output**

I read the duty board beside the gate, then ask the nearest guard,

The unfamiliar person answers in a level voice: "I am."

They turn from the board to face me directly. "Captain Ysra Vale."

They nod toward the duty board.

## Turn 3

**Input**

"Captain Vale, I'm Rowan Hale, a surveyor. What is Aldermere most short of today?" I ask.

**Output**

The unfamiliar person's measured words hang between us after I speak, their voice carrying clear across the gatehouse.

"A surveyor? We could use better maps of the eastern ridge. What's your rate?"

They turn from the gate ledger to face me squarely, silver-braided insignia catching the lit morning light. The duty board still lists them as commander, alongside the sharp-faced guard eyeing the road, Lira Morn with her lantern, and Tormac Reed bearing his cheek scar. Beyond the open door to Market Square, the low roofs and bustle wait.

## Turn 4

**Input**

"Meals and a bed while I work," I answer. "Which problem would you send a surveyor to first, and why?"

**Output**

I offer meals and a bed while I work, then ask which problem they'd send a surveyor to first, and why.

The unfamiliar person answers in a practical voice, "The collapsed bridge on the east road. A surveyor can map the damage and the detour before the next rain turns the ford into a swamp."

Their words settle between us in the lit gatehouse, the wooden board still bolted to the stone wall beside the open doorway. Beyond it the path to Market Square stands ready, morning air stirring faintly with open-air scents.

## Turn 5

**Input**

"I'll inspect the bridge. When did it collapse, and who actually saw it happen?" I ask Captain Vale.

**Output**

The heavy stones of the North Gate press cool and solid against my shoulder as I speak, my question cutting across the lit gatehouse to Captain Ysra Vale.

Captain Vale's measured voice replies, "This morning, just after dawn. The surveyor here saw it first."

She turns her gaze to me, silver-braided insignia catching the light.

## Turn 6

**Input**

"I only arrived moments ago," I say. "Which surveyor do you mean?"

**Output**

I speak, and the words land in the lit gatehouse like a thrown stone.

The unfamiliar person says in a level voice: "The one standing right there with the dusty cloak and the badger clasp. Claims he was sent by the cartographer’s guild in High Hollow."

They nod once toward me.

## Turn 7

**Input**

I look down at my own clothes, then meet her eyes. "You are pointing at me, Captain. I have no badger clasp, I belong to no guild, and I did not witness the bridge collapse. Are you confusing me with someone else?"

**Output**

I look down at my own clothes, then meet her eyes as I speak, the words landing heavy between us in the morning air.

"My mistake. The clasp is on the surveyor behind you."

The unfamiliar person's even tone carries no edge, just a flat correction that shifts the weight of the moment. The wooden board on the stone wall still lists the on-duty roster beside the gatehouse entrance, and beyond the open doorway the path to Market Square waits.
