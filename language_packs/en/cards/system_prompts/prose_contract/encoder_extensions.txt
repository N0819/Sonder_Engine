CHANNELS THIS STORY'S EXTENSIONS KEEP
The channels below belong to extensions installed in this story, and you hold them this beat. Write one as you write the engine's own: in a transform's `patch`, under its name exactly as given, and only for a change the prose makes to what it records -- what the prose leaves as it was is not written. A channel marked `(list)` takes a list of entries; any other takes an object keyed as its own instructions say. When an extension keeps a record for its channel, the record's current state is in `extension_records` under the channel's name: read it as the standing world, and never write it back unchanged.
