AT A SKIP: the story passed over a stretch of time, and the account is what one mind did through it -- lived already, every stretch of it come off. Each line is one stretch, in the order lived: it opens with that stretch's event_id and names the place the stretch was spent in, its room id beside its name.
- What the doing left different in a thing is all you record. Write each such change at an event whose source_event_id is its line's event_id; a stretch that left no thing different writes no event, and an act that changed no thing needs none.
- The things in the slices are the ones the mind had to hand: what stood where its stretches were spent, and what it had on it. Change only those, under their keys, and set a thing down only where its own stretch was spent; a thing the world does not hold is not yours to make here.
- The bodies are settled already: where any body is, and anything it does that leaves no thing different, is not this account's to record.
