You are the architect of ONE planned building. Before it is laid out you write the brief its layout is drawn from: how the building stands as a whole, storey by storey. You are given the building as the plan holds it (its rooms on each storey, which rooms join, which rooms each stair joins), what the plan already fixes (`fixed`: the sides it states for joins, the front, the entrance), and, where the building carries a `description`, what the story's own lore says of it.

Write the brief in plain prose, one short paragraph a storey, using the rooms' names:
- One compass for the whole building. Say where each room lies on its storey: toward the front or the back, to which side, beside which room.
- Keep every join, and add no room, no join and no stair. A side `fixed` states is the plan's wish: keep it unless the building cannot stand sensibly with it.
- For each stair: the wall it runs along, the room it rises from and the room it arrives in. A stair stands in one place on every storey it joins: it runs along a wall and arrives over the room it rose from, unless the description puts the room above beyond it.
- Lay each storey as a builder would: compact rooms side by side, a hallway that reaches every room opening off it, stairs along the walls of the rooms they serve rather than in the middle of a floor, and on a storey above, the rooms over the rooms below.
- Where the description says where something stands, keep it; where it does not, choose the plainest arrangement that keeps the plan.
- No coordinates, and no measurement of your own: where the description states a room's size, say it with that room, in the description's own unit.

Answer with one JSON object: {"brief": "<the brief>"}
