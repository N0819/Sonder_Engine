PHYSICAL TRANSFORMATION -- a body that has actually CHANGED (shapeshifted, hexed into another form, grown or lost a feature): kind:'physical_transformation'. It is NOT a disguise: there is no concealed truth and nobody sees through it, so never give it known_to or concealed_terms. Inside state give (a) form -- a short name for what the body now is ('a red fox'); (b) appearance -- the body's new TRUE visible description, which REPLACES the card's outright; (c) parts -- the complete list of extra body parts the new body has, each {kind, count, at, aspect}; omit parts to leave the card's alone, or give [] for a form that has none. Add caused_by when somebody else did this to them.
REVERSIBILITY IS THE DEFAULT AND A ONE-WAY DOOR IS A DECISION. Omit `reversible` and the shape can be dropped again; set reversible:false ONLY when the fiction means the subject is genuinely stuck, and then say in `reversal` how it could ever end, if anything can -- trapping somebody by forgetting a field is the one mistake here nobody can undo. Give expires_at_seconds when it wears off on its own. To END a transformation, re-emit it with active:false: the engine clears every transformation on that body, so you need not know the ids of any you did not write.
A transformed body may ALSO be disguised: the transformation settles what the body IS, and the disguise hides it.

PHYSICAL DISGUISE -- a body concealing its real appearance (a shapeshifter hiding anomalous features, a spy in another's uniform, an illusion): kind:'physical_disguise', and inside state (a) presented_appearance -- a positive description of what an observer who does NOT know the truth sees (never the hidden feature, not even as 'concealed'); (b) concealed_terms -- the feature words to keep out of unaware views, drawn from what THIS body is hiding (a pair of wings, a brand, a regiment's insignia, a species name); (c) known_to -- the characters who legitimately KNOW the real form (witnessed the change, were told); and (d) conceals_identity -- true ONLY when the disguise covers what this body is RECOGNISED by: its face, build, bearing or voice, as a mask, a full illusion or another's uniform does. A disguise that hides or alters a feature and leaves the recognisable ones untouched is not that -- anyone who already knows this person still knows them. Omit the field and recognition survives. A disguise conceals appearance only, never the subject's senses or abilities.
