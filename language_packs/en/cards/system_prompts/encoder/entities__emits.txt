LIGHT: anything that gives light is an entity with `light_source` set to what it EMITS (dim|lit|bright) -- a torch, a lantern, a phone screen, a campfire someone just built, a burning wreck, a glowing rune. Building a fire is creating that entity with a position, and state.lit false is one gone out. A carried light travels with whoever holds it. `light_radius` is 'spot' (a pool -- a hand torch, a candle) or 'room' (it fills the space -- a hearth, a ceiling lamp); portable things default to a pool, and a pool lights only its holder and whoever stands with them -- everyone else in that room is a shape in the dark who can see the light without being in it.
A SOURCE IS A CLASS, NOT A DEVICE: a thing that gives off light or sound carries the level it gives -- `light_source` (dim|lit|bright), `sound_source` (faint|audible|loud|deafening|thunderous|catastrophic), either or both -- and its switch: state.lit false for a light gone out, state.running false for a sound stopped. A light also carries `light_shape` -- 'cone' when its light has a direction, 'all_round' when it has none -- and state.pointed_at naming the entity, anchor, BODY or compass bearing a cone is aimed at, a carried one included, because an arm aims independently of a chest. It must be a NAME the scene holds or a bearing word: a phrase resolves to nothing, and the cone falls back to wherever its holder faces. Where a beam is held on a body, NAME THE BODY. `light_height` (floor|waist|head|full) is where the light sits, which decides what casts a shadow. `steadiness` 'flickering' or 'failing' marks one that cannot be relied on: the engine takes it down a step or puts it out, and a thing that both lights and hums fails as one.
AN EMISSION IS A STATE; A NOISE IS AN EVENT. sound_source with state.running says the thing is emitting from now until something stops it, masking quieter voices in its room and next door through every later beat. A thing that MADE a sound this beat is not MAKING one: the test is whether somebody would have to do something to make it stop. If nothing would, it is a sensory_events entry, and state.running stays false. HOW FAR A SOUND GOES IS HOW LOUD IT IS: only the top two rungs carry past the building a sound is made in, and where no word reaches it, put the number on `db` beside the level.
SMELL: `scent` is a short noun phrase for the smell a thing standingly gives off -- a hot oven, lamp oil, a body three days dead, a machine running hot. It belongs to the object, not the beat: a smell that flares and passes is a sensory event, and matter that landed somewhere and stayed is substance_ops. Set it only where a nose in that room would meet it; most things have none. A redeclared entity silent on scent keeps its scent; change it when the thing changes, as a fire dies or meat turns.
