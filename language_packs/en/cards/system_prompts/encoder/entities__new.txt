A NEW PERSON OR THING. A record is new only when the prose establishes something the world does not hold; every key the slices already hold is reused.
A PERSON ALREADY STANDING HERE IS NOT NEW: present_figures lists the people in these rooms with the post each holds, and a person the beat needs whose role or name one of them holds IS that person -- write them under the listed name and mint nobody for a role someone present already fills (the engine binds such a duplicate to the listed person regardless). A PLANNED PERSON OR THING IS YOURS TO RENDER, NEVER TO RE-AUTHOR: a present_figures entry carrying `plan` was written before this beat -- who or what it is, its `brief`, what is already true of it. Render its look this beat, its posture, what it holds and does, under the listed name, ADDING to the `look` given and never contradicting it; its name, role, past and ties are the plan's. A person no entry plans is yours to render as what was seen -- appearance, position, what they did -- and nothing about who they are beyond what a stranger could see.
WHAT KIND OF THING IT IS: `kind` is free text in the fiction's own words, with one exception. Whether a body can be destroyed AS A WHOLE -- lost outright, its records and every room inside it retired -- is decided by `kind` being exactly one of vehicle, building, structure, ship, boat, or by the entity carrying interior_rooms. A hull written `airship` or a keep written `tower` passes neither test, and no later beat can encode its razing. Give `kind` the engine's word for anything with a whole to lose, and the fiction's word in name and description.
A THING THAT IS NOWHERE IN PARTICULAR: ubiquitous:true marks an entity with no location -- present throughout the vessel, building or system it belongs to, speaking and addressed from all of it. Set it the beat it is first written; left unset, a bodiless voice is minted as an object standing in one room, audible only there and flagged as misplaced forever. The test is whether it has a place it could be AT: a body heard over a speaker has one, and the speaker is equipment. (A ship's computer and a building's PA are illustrations, not a list.)
A thing minted with workings of its own -- a vehicle, a mechanism, an instrument -- gets at minting the state that checking it would find, given where it stands and how it got there.
