THINGS: entities records the objects the scene holds -- what each is and what condition it is in. A redeclared entity merges onto its record by id, and state you do not mention is kept.

STATE IS ONE BODY'S OWN DOING AND CONDITION -- never what a DIFFERENT body is doing to it, in it or inside it. 'crate shut over the figure prying at its hinge' writes a second body's ACT into the first body's record; write 'crate shut, faintly shifting', and put the other body's act in its own record. This is not tidiness: an enclosed body's state is withheld from minds with no channel to it, but the CONTAINER stands in plain view, so an act written into its record reaches every observer who can see it, however sealed the enclosure.
STATE IS CONFIGURATION THAT OUTLASTS THE BEAT: open, worn, held, lit, in transit. What a thing is doing this instant -- a motion, a sound it is making, a taste or a feeling it registers -- is an event, not a state key; such keys expire when the story moves on.
WHERE A THING IS IS NEVER A STATE KEY. A place -- inside or under another thing and out of sight included -- is kept by the placing tools (inventory_ops, stations, containment). A state word saying a thing is hidden, stowed or concealed is read by NOTHING: the thing goes on being seen and smelled by everyone in reach, and no later beat can encode its discovery.
WHETHER A WAY THROUGH IS OPEN IS NEVER A STATE KEY EITHER. A door between two rooms is that doorway's edge, and its barrier alone decides what passes: an entity for the same door is scenery -- what it is made of, how it is hung, warped, rusted, scorched -- and never open, ajar, shut, sealed or dogged, because nothing that decides passage reads that, and the page would be handed both answers at once. A lid, a hatch, or the door of a thing with an inside -- a box, a cupboard, a vehicle -- IS this record's: its state.hatch opens and shuts it.
ONE RECORD: what a thing shows and what is true of it live on the thing itself, never as a second copy on another entity.

Some changes to things have parts of their own, shipped when the prose was judged to need them: entities__new (a person or thing brought into the scene), entities__inside (a thing somebody can be inside, and its lid), entities__emits (light, sound and smell), entities__transit (a vehicle, lift or vessel under way) and entities__examined (a thing looked at closely, read, or damaged). When the prose does one of those and its part is not below, ask for the part in missing_tools.

Shape: entities:{entity_id:{name,kind,description,aliases:[],portable,container,interior_rooms:[],state:{},scent,ubiquitous}} -- a redeclared entity merges onto the existing record by id; state is that body's OWN doing only.
