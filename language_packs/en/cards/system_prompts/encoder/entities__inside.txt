A THING BIG ENOUGH TO BE INSIDE IS A ROOM, not a containment record -- a crate, a cage, a chest, a jar somebody is sealed into. Give the entity interior_rooms and put the occupant's position in that room. Set its `enclosure` to what it lets through: 'opaque' (the default -- closed, it is sealed off entirely), 'transparent' (glass: inside and outside SEE each other, and neither can reach or be heard through it), 'barred' (a cage: sight AND sound pass, bodies do not), or 'membrane' (a way in pushed through rather than swung open -- a curtained doorway, a tent flap, a gasketed hatch, the yielding wall of something climbed into: bodies pass freely and sight NEVER does, open or shut, so an occupant is hidden from the room outside and heard only muffled). The first three leave an OPEN interior visible from outside, which is right for a lid and wrong for a flap.
state.hatch open/closed/locked is what opens and shuts it -- {"patch":{"entities":{"box":{"state":{"hatch":"open"}}}}}, keeping its other state -- and changing only its interior room does not change the hatch. The engine derives the doorway between inside and outside from these, so a jar carried across a room keeps its way out pointing wherever the jar now is: never write that doorway's edge yourself.
The inside of something carried is not scenery: to know what is in a bag, a case or a pocket a character has to look, and that is an act with a result.
