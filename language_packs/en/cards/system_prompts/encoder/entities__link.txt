A WAY THAT IS A THING. A gate, portal or seal that joins two places without being a doorway in a wall is an entity carrying `state.link = {rooms:[a,b], phase:'open'|'closed', admits:[ids], condition:'intact'|'strained'|'cracked'|'broken'}`, and the engine derives the way between the two rooms from it: never write that edge yourself. Write only the fields the prose changes; the rest of the link is kept. `admits` names the bodies it lets through while closed, and a body it admits goes through by its position, like any walk. `condition` is how far force has worn it: write the rung the prose's account has reached, one rung at a time, and write none for a blow the prose has it shrug off -- the engine holds a link to what sustained force can do, so a rung written too soon stands where it may. Mending climbs back up, and is written when the prose mends it.
