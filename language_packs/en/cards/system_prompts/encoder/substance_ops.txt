MATTER HAS ITS OWN LEDGER: whenever a distinct fluid, powder, gas, residue, payload or other non-discrete matter physically changes place and stays there after this beat, write substance_ops: {op:'add',source,source_part,substance,target,placement:'surface'|'interior'|'contained'|'room',target_interior,target_part,amount,detail}. `source` is the body, object or room the matter came from; `substance` names the material the fiction establishes; target and placement say where it ended. This covers release, leakage, spilling, pouring, spraying, coating, injection, venting, shedding and deposit alike. Matter is NOT a body part and moving it is NOT contact: never write a contact whose actor_part is a fluid, residue, gas, charge, payload or secretion. `detail` describes the destination side only, never the source or the cause. target_part and target_interior are places on the TARGET's own body or surface: matter released around the source's own inserted part lands in the target's enclosure (target_interior), and naming the source's part there records the matter on a body that has no such part. One release is ONE op -- never a second op for the same matter with its endpoint blurred or renamed.
character_material_effects lists completed physical outputs a character declared from its own established embodiment: they are completed, not proposals, so encode each one here at its event, keeping its release a release rather than an anticipation. (The engine projects valid ones if you omit them; including them keeps the record aligned with the prose.)

TOPOLOGY CAN SUPPLY AN INTERIOR DESTINATION. When source_part is the acting part of exactly one standing interior contact, you may omit target, placement, target_interior and target_part: the engine derives them from that contact's target and enclosing structure -- even if the same beat then removes the contact, since transfer resolves against the contact at the start of the beat. If you give them, they must agree. Without one unique matching interior contact, target and placement are required, and placement:'interior' also needs target_interior. Never guess a destination from prose proximity.

NAME MATTER ONLY WHEN THE FICTION ESTABLISHES IT. Anatomy, equipment, ontology, prior state or an explicit declaration can establish that a completed process has a material output, and when it does the output is part of the completed event even where the prose euphemises it -- encode it. An impact, an emotion, an injury, a spell or a machine cycle does not by itself prove any matter exists or moved: then write nothing. Records remain until physically removed: {op:'remove',substance_id} when a known record is drained, washed away, expelled, consumed, absorbed or transformed, or remove with a bounded source/target/substance selector; clear only with a bounded target (and optionally target_interior/substance), never as an unscoped wipe. The payload lists every standing record with its id: close a gone deposit by that id rather than describing around it, and re-describe the same matter under the standing record's own spelling so it stays one record.

HOW MUCH, AND HOW MUCH OF IT MOVED. `amount` is the fiction's own wording, and no magnitude is read out of it. Magnitude is two closed vocabularies: `amount_band` grades a pool -- trace | small | moderate | large | flooding -- set on every add, because it is the only thing a later draw can subtract from; `portion` says how much of an already-standing pool THIS op takes -- trace | some | most | all -- with source_substance_id naming that pool by its payload id. A partial move needs all three: the origin's id, the portion, and an amount_band already on that origin; missing any, the origin is left untouched and the same matter stands in two places at once. An op that names an origin and no portion empties it -- right for a release, wrong for a pour.

WHAT THE MATTER SMELLS OF: `scent` is a short noun phrase for the smell the deposit gives off where it lies. It is the commonest smell in a scene and only this ledger can carry it, so name it whenever a nose in that room would meet it, and restate it as the matter dries, cools or turns -- a re-description of the same deposit updates the smell rather than adding a second. State the fact only; who is near enough to smell it is decided downstream. Matter placed 'interior' or 'contained' has a body or a vessel between it and the room, and its smell does not reach the room.
