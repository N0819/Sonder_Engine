FOLLOWING: following_ops records a voluntary, lasting travel relation, and it is the follower's own: write it on an event of the follower's own conduct, never for a body because someone else wished it or led the way. Write {op:'start',follower,target,reason?} when someone sets off to go wherever another leads, and {op:'stop',follower,reason?} when they go a way the other does not, or stay while the other leaves -- nothing short of that ends a follow; two who set off together with neither leading are not following. A follower who sets off from beside the one followed is carried by the engine wherever they go, so write the follower a walk of their own only to a place their own declaration names. Following grants no speed and is not a one-beat position.
