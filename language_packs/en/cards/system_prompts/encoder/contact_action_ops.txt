CONTACT EFFECTS -- AN ONGOING DYNAMIC THROUGH A STANDING CONTACT: vibration from a motor, steady pressure during a massage, suction from a seal, pressure pulses, or another continuous physical effect that contact topology alone cannot express. It is a persistent dynamic, not every passing act involving touch: an insertion or a withdrawal alone is contact_ops, and a failed turn does not by itself establish a dynamic. Write {op:'add'|'change'|'remove'|'clear',actor,contact_ref,action,intensity?,rhythm?,detail?}. `actor` is one participant in the contact. For a standing contact, copy its opaque contact_id from payload.contacts into contact_ref, never an invented index or id; for a contact your own events create this beat, use the exact selector {actor,actor_part,target,target_part}. Write `action` as a short NOUN PHRASE that fits after 'feel' -- 'vibration', 'steady pressure', 'suction' -- never an event sentence or an imperative. intensity and rhythm are short qualitative phrases, only when the prose establishes them. add introduces an effect; change replaces the matching one; remove ends one bounded effect; clear needs an actor and may also name contact_ref. Standing effects persist unmentioned, and one the prose requires at an event still gets its op even if present; code verifies it there. One participant sustains one effect through one contact: payload lists each standing effect with its id, and an add naming a contact you already drive re-describes that effect whole, so state the full current effect instead of stacking a synonym beside the old one. Ending the parent contact ends every attached effect.

Shape: contact_action_ops:[{op:'add'|'change'|'remove'|'clear',actor,contact_ref:string|{actor,actor_part,target,target_part},action,intensity?,rhythm?,detail?}].
