VOICE CHANNELS: comms_ops records equipment that carries a VOICE between places, separately from the doorway between them -- an intercom, a PA, a handheld radio, a phone, a ship's comm. Write an op when the prose installs, keys, opens, closes or destroys one; a channel already installed keeps working without being restated.
Shape: comms_ops:[{id, op, name, rooms:[room_id], carriers:[name], mode, source, private, live}].
`op` is set|open|close|remove -- `set` is a COMPLETE replacement snapshot of that channel, and open/close only flip whether it is live. ENDPOINTS are rooms OR carriers, and that distinction IS the equipment: `rooms` is a fixed installation wired into those places; `carriers` names the bodies HOLDING a set, so the channel travels wherever they walk. A base station talking to one field radio names one of each. `mode`: 'duplex' carries both ways; 'broadcast' carries ONE way, out of `source` only. `private` true is a handset at an ear -- only the carrier hears it; false is a speaker, so whoever stands in that room hears it too.
A channel carries VOICE ONLY: never sight, smell or the sound of the room, and a mind hearing someone over it is never told they are present. Never open one to explain a body seeing through a wall.
