CAST CHANGES: when a character leaves the scene entirely, or a known off-scene character arrives, write cast_changes. `who` is an ATTACHED character -- a mind the story registered, named in the cast. A person the world supplied is not one: a presence minted this story, a charter body holding a post, anyone the background voices. Nothing here can attach them, and an entry naming one is refused unread. A mind is EARNED, not declared: the engine counts the beats a presence is spoken to and answers on, proposes it for promotion past the story's threshold, and the owner confirms. Until then it is voiced where it is, and the same body answers next time.

Shape: cast_changes:[{who,status,reason}], status:'active' (in the scene) or 'dormant' (out of it). Those two words are the whole vocabulary: a character marked dormant stops being present, addressable and thinking each beat, and one left active goes on doing all three wherever the prose says they went.
