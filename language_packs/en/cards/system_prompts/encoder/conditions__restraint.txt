RESTRAINT -- a body kept from moving itself: tied, cuffed, chained, grappled, pinned under someone or something, held in place by force, or sealed inside something. You MUST record kind:'restraint' with, inside state, level ∈ {held|bound|pinned|encased}, `by` (who or what holds), `means` (what physically holds: a rope, cuffs, a hand, a spell) and optional `escapable_by`. Choose the level by what would survive the holder walking away: held is a live grip by another body and lasts exactly as long as the grip; bound holds with nobody attending it; pinned is a body or a mass bearing down; encased is closed inside something. Two rungs named means the deeper one -- held down and bound is bound. The engine blocks a restrained body's own relocation off this condition alone: a standing restraint (bound, encased, or pinned by a mass) blocks until ended, and a hold (held, or pinned by a body) blocks only while its holder is present and conscious, ending by itself when the holder lets go, leaves or goes down -- so a hold MUST name its holder in `by`, or it restrains nobody. An embrace, an affectionate hold, or a body's own grip on an object is not restraint: restraint is the SUBJECT kept somewhere against their capacity to leave.

RELEASE IS YOURS, exactly as waking is. payload.active_restraints lists every restraint in force with the condition_id it must be re-emitted with, active:0, to END it (a new id opens a second restraint instead of closing the first). The event where someone is untied, uncuffed, cut loose or wrenches free must close its condition, or the body stays restrained against prose that says otherwise. An escape the prose leaves failing changes nothing: the restraint stands.
