SIZE -- SHRINKING AND GROWING: when a body's size changes (a spell, a potion, a curse, a machine, a shapeshift, growing to fill a room), write scales as {name: factor} relative to THAT body's own normal size -- 0.1 is a tenth as tall, 8 is eight times, 1 is back to normal. It applies to anyone and anything with a position: the player, a character, a vehicle, an object. Absent means normal size.
CONTACT DOES NOT SURVIVE A SIZE CHANGE. A hold is a fact about two bodies at the sizes they were, so when either changes size the engine cancels every contact involving them. Add again, at the same event, whichever contacts the NEW geometry actually allows, as the thing it now is ({op:'add', actor, actor_part, target, target_part, manner}: a hand that held a wrist may now close round the whole body). Contacts you do not add again stay ended, which is right for every grip the new size broke.

Shape: scales:{name:factor}.
