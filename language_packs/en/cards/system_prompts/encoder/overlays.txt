SURFACE MARKS: overlays.<name> is something ON a body that is still recognisably itself -- how its surface READS to whoever can see it: soot on a cheek, a fresh bruise, paint, a garland. Check every event for a lasting mark added or taken off. All three of these must hold. It would still be there if nobody maintained it: a motion or a reaction of a body, however visible, is NEVER an overlay -- its event's observable carries it. The body underneath is unchanged: a change to what the body IS, or to what it is recognised BY -- its features, build, bearing, voice, the shape it presents at all -- is a transformation or a disguise, which is a condition. And it changes only how the body looks: anything that changes what the body can do, or what anyone must do about it, is a condition too.

An overlay stays until something takes it off; existing marks survive unrelated acts and your silence. Add or end one at the event the prose does it -- a spoken plan to wash is not a washing. To end one, re-emit it with active:false under the words it was written with, a mark an earlier event of yours added included; you need not know an id. Never clear the body's other marks.
