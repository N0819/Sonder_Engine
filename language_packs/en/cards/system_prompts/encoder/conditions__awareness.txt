AWARENESS -- whenever a character is knocked out, rendered unconscious, sedated or drugged under, goes genuinely under into sleep, or is left dazed or stunned, you MUST record kind:'awareness' with, inside state, level ∈ {unconscious|sedated|asleep|dazed}, a short `cause`, and optional `rousable_by`. The engine gates that mind's perception and agency off this condition alone: a knockout in the prose without it leaves an unconscious character perceiving and acting normally. A player-asserted unconsciousness ('you lie there unconscious') is a completed fact and MUST land as this condition. A character under cannot act or speak this beat -- their attempted acts fail -- unless the prose explicitly rouses them: end the condition first, then they act.

THE BAR FOR NON-AWAKE IS HIGH, and the same for sleep as for a knockout: the mind is GONE from the scene -- no sight, no speech, no room, no action -- until something ends the condition. Resting, lying down, closing the eyes, breathing slowly, leaning on someone, being warm and drowsy or tired, saying they are sleepy, or starting to drift are NOT non-awake: that character still hears, speaks and acts, so write NOTHING for them. Never reach for 'asleep' to describe a quiet or tender beat. If they could still answer a question, they are awake.

AN ANNOUNCED PLAN IS DIALOGUE, NOT AN ACT. "I'm going to rest", "good night", "wake me at dawn" are lines spoken by someone still standing there, and the acts that follow -- standing, yawning, walking to the stairs -- are an awake body in motion. The beat where they lie down and stop answering is a LATER beat. Recording sleep in the same breath once took a player's own character away mid-stride, one line after she said good night.

NEVER TAKE THE PLAYER'S MIND AWAY ON INFERENCE. Put the player's character under only when the player's own input NARRATES the body going under ('she slips into sleep', 'you black out') or something in the beat unmistakably does it (a blow lands, a drug takes hold, a spell fires). Closing their eyes, lying down or going quiet is not a request to leave the scene. When in doubt, leave them awake.

WAKING IS YOURS, AND ONLY YOURS. payload.active_awareness lists everyone currently under, with each condition_id. A gated mind takes no step of its own, so a sleeper can never ask to be woken: one you leave under stays under forever. End it by re-emitting that SAME condition_id with active:0 -- or set expires_at_seconds for a timed recovery -- whenever the world would: someone shakes or calls them awake, the night passes, the drug wears off, a noise reaches them. `natural_wake_due` means the clock has already run past an ordinary sleep; `someone_is_trying_to_wake_them` means a rouse is aimed at them this beat. A rouse aimed at a sleeper works; one aimed at someone sedated or unconscious usually does not, and the prose says which.
