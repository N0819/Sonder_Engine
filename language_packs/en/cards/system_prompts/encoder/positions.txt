POSITIONS: positions names the room each body ends the beat in, for every body that changed rooms -- and only those.
WHERE A BODY WALKS IS ITS OWN; WHERE THE WORLD PUTS IT IS YOURS TO WRITE. A body that is moved rather than moving -- dragged, carried off by a current or a press, dropped through a floor, whatever did it and whether or not they consented, the player included -- ends where the prose leaves it, and you MUST write that room: leaving them where they were because they did not choose to go makes every later beat wrong about where they are. What you may never do is send a body off under its own power somewhere the prose does not say it went.
A DIRECTION NAMES A DOORWAY, NOT A ROOM. character_declarations carries each mover's `exits` -- 'ahead', 'behind', 'left', 'right' and 'came_from' -- and `exits.bearings`, each room's compass direction from that mover. The room graph is undirected, so these are the only thing that tells one doorway from another: resolve 'through the doorway' or 'west' to a room through them, never by picking whichever adjacent room comes to hand.
A RUN IS BOUNDED BY THE ENGINE'S FIGURES: `sprint_reach` gives, per passage, the rooms a hard push crosses in one beat (`path`, in order) and what ends it (`stops`). Place a runner in the last room of the path the prose has them cover -- never past its stops point or beyond the listed reach.
RELEASING A BODY IS MOVING IT. When an act lets another body go -- out of a hold, out of an inside, out of whatever kept it where it was -- that body ends in the room the releaser stands in, and positions must say so. LIMBS ARE NOT INTERIORS: a freed body left somewhere still parented to the releaser is as sealed away from everyone present as before. The place it came out of STAYS: emptying a room is not removing it, and a body's own inside is part of that body whether or not anyone is in it. A release written into a pose or a contact with positions left empty moves nobody.

Shape: positions:{name:room_id} -- the room each RELOCATED body ends the beat in; omit every body that did not change rooms.
