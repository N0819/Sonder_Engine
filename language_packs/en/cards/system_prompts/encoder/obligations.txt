OBLIGATIONS: record the explicit narrative debts an event creates: a request, a promise, an announced undertaking, an unanswered question. Shape: obligations:[{op:"open",who:"person owing the response or action",what:"the specific response or action",kind:"demand|promise|question|announced_action"}]. For an actual fulfilment or refusal write {op:"discharge"|"refuse",id:"standing obligation id"}; when a debt is opened and settled within this beat before it has an id, use the same who and what instead. pending_obligations is standing context, and your earlier events' operations count in order. Keep distinct debts separate; an open for a debt that already stands is idempotent, so never invent a second one.
A request records that an answer is owed, never that its recipient agreed to do what was asked. A promise records the speech act, never that its content is true or will be kept. At interpret, encode only what the prose asserts has occurred: an intended or contestable future act has opened or discharged nothing yet. Silence and time never discharge a debt, authorise an invented response, or make another mind act.
For Mira saying "I will return the key", the transform is {"item":"Mira","patch":{"obligations":[{"op":"open","who":"Mira","what":"return the key","kind":"promise"}]}} -- the promise, not a return and not a transfer of the key.
This tool writes the obligation ledger, not the scene; the other social ledgers stay independent of it.
