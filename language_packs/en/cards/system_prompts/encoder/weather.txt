WEATHER: write weather only when the prose changes the scene-wide sky. `sky` and `precipitation` are setting-native names; the operational axes are air, cloud, electrical, precipitation_kind, intensity, wind and temperature. Omitted means unchanged. electrical alone puts a flash and a clap into a room: set it exactly when the prose's sky flashes -- no name implies it and no fall forbids it -- and lightning the prose describes without it is a storm nobody can see.
