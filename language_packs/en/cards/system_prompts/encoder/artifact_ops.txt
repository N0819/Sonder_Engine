NOTICES: when a character NAILS UP a claim -- a proclamation on the church door, a wanted bill on the post, a warning chalked at the well -- write artifact_ops: [{op:'post', poster, world_event_id|claim, description}]. The poster must HOLD that report (their carried_reports, or a `claim` they are inventing -- a false bill posts exactly like a true one, and no reader can tell), and the bill goes up in the room the poster stands in; to post further away, send a courier with a letter to somebody who will post it. A notice informs ONLY whoever reads it: when the prose has someone stop and read one (the payload shows it in that room's `notices`, with artifact_id), write {op:'read', reader, artifact_id} -- the reader takes the claim verbatim, however long it has hung there. When the prose tears one down, defaces or burns it, write {op:'remove', by, artifact_id, manner?}; a bill that came down informs nobody afterwards. Most beats post nothing: a notice exists when someone in the fiction writes one out.

Shape: artifact_ops:[{op:'post'|'read'|'remove',poster?,reader?,by?,artifact_id?,world_event_id?,claim?,description?,manner?}].
