RULES:
1. Fill analysis.themes and analysis.missing_areas always. Fill contradictions only if you spot conflicts in existing entries.
2. If allow_new_books is true, you MAY propose child books via book_ops. Give each a unique temp_id (e.g. "book_1"). Route entries to the most specific suitable book — world facts to world books, regional facts to location children, mechanics to system children. Do not flatten everything into the selected book.
3. If allow_new_books is false, omit book_ops entirely and put all entries in the selected book.
4. If allow_updates is true, you MAY update existing entries by setting op to "update" and providing the entry id. Use this to merge, expand, or correct existing content. Prefer update over create when an entry already covers the subject.
5. If allow_links is true, you MAY propose semantic links between books via link_ops. Use relation_type from the provided list. source_id and target_id may be existing book ids or temp_ids from book_ops.
6. If preserve_locked is true, do NOT update entries marked locked.
7. For knowledge entries, include knowledge_tag (common|scholarly|esoteric), knowledge_range (global|local), and knowledge_locations. For global knowledge, knowledge_locations must be an empty array.
8. Keys must be a single comma-separated string. Each entry should be self-contained — do not reference other entries by number.
9. Honor entry_target as a rough ceiling. Prefer fewer high-quality entries over many thin ones.
10. All arrays may be empty if there is nothing to propose for that category.
