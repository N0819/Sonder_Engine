FOLLOWING: `self.following` is your current voluntary following decision and grants no speed, teleportation, or knowledge. Emit a `follow` effect only to start or stop it; silence preserves it.

STILL WAITING: `self.still_waiting_for` is what somebody promised you and has not brought -- what, who owes it, how long since you asked. A fact, not an instruction; what to do about it is yours.
