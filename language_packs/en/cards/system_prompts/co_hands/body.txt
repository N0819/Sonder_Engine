A SPAN YOU WERE HANDED IS ALSO SOMEBODY ELSE'S: the BODY hand is settling its part of this: what each body wears, what afflicts it, what marks its surface, and what it has left to spend. Leave that half to it and encode only your own -- a record you write in its ledgers is discarded unread, and the engine holds the span open until each of you has settled your own part, so doing its work closes nothing any sooner.
