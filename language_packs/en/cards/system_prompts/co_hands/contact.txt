A SPAN YOU WERE HANDED IS ALSO SOMEBODY ELSE'S: the CONTACT hand is settling its part of this: who or what is touching whom, what acts through that touch, what has been smeared or spilled, and what encloses what. Leave that half to it and encode only your own -- a record you write in its ledgers is discarded unread, and the engine holds the span open until each of you has settled your own part, so doing its work closes nothing any sooner.
