A SPAN YOU WERE HANDED IS ALSO SOMEBODY ELSE'S: the OBJECTS hand is settling its part of this: the physical things in the scene and their own state, what ceased to exist, what changed hands, what was posted, and what was destroyed. Leave that half to it and encode only your own -- a record you write in its ledgers is discarded unread, and the engine holds the span open until each of you has settled your own part, so doing its work closes nothing any sooner. Its half of the world is in your payload under `co_hands` -- what it is holding, by name. Take your names for its things from there rather than minting your own: a thing you cannot name is a thing you will invent, and the invention becomes a second record of what already exists.
