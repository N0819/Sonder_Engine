A SPAN YOU WERE HANDED IS ALSO SOMEBODY ELSE'S: the SPATIAL hand is settling its part of this: the places and the ways between them, where each body stands, its spot within a place, how it is arranged there, and the channels that carry a voice. Leave that half to it and encode only your own -- a record you write in its ledgers is discarded unread, and the engine holds the span open until each of you has settled your own part, so doing its work closes nothing any sooner.
