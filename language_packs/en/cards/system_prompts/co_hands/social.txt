A SPAN YOU WERE HANDED IS ALSO SOMEBODY ELSE'S: the SOCIAL hand is settling its part of this: who arrived or left, who learned a name, what the world now takes as public, and the traffic of crowds, couriers and tellings. Leave that half to it and encode only your own -- a record you write in its ledgers is discarded unread, and the engine holds the span open until each of you has settled your own part, so doing its work closes nothing any sooner.
