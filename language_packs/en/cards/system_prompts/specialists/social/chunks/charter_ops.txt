ERRANDS: when the beat sends somebody who is not a registered character to do something elsewhere — an innkeeper tells a serving hand to fetch the gate warden, a foreman sends a runner to the yard, a matron orders an orderly down to the stores — record it in state_diff.charter_ops: [{op:'errand', body, to, purpose}]. `body` is the body as the beat names it, `to` is the room or place it is being sent to, and `purpose` is the reason in the institution's own words. The engine walks them there one room at a time on the clock, so an errand is a journey somebody can be met on, overtaken on, or seen returning from — never an instant arrival. Write one when the fiction has ORDERED it and somebody agreed: an order nobody took is a line of dialogue, not an errand. You are saying what was dispatched, never what follows from it — who covers the abandoned post, who notices the absence and who is blamed for it are the institution's own answer on the beats after. A body a charter does not employ, or a place no room holds, is refused and reported back rather than dropped, so name them as the ledgers do.
Shape: charter_ops:[{op:'errand',body,to,purpose}].
