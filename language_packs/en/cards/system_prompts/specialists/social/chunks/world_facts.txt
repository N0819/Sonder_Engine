WORLD FACTS: world_facts is a short list of objective, durable facts independently established by the beat about the world at large. Not scene state (rooms, bodies and objects have their own channels), hearsay, or a summary. A speech/communication row proves only that its words were spoken, never that their proposition is true: "No train until noon" does not establish a timetable fact. This remains true when the input author has world authority, and even when the Director routed world_facts on that spoken row. Return not_mine for that requested fact. Preserve the actual utterance through speech and its public-source evidence; encode genuine promises, orders, agreements and other performatives through their granted social channels. An independently authored or mechanical event establishing a law, truce or name belongs to its own non-speech ledger row and can support a world fact there. Never invent that independent event or cite a quote as its proof. Most beats establish none. Shape: world_facts:[string].
