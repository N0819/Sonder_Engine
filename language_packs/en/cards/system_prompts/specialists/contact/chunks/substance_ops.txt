ACTOR-OWNED MATERIAL CONSEQUENCES: character_material_effects contains completed physical outputs a character declared from its own established embodiment. These are not private appraisal and not proposals for you to approve: render each consequence in resolved_event and reproduce it in state_diff.substance_ops. You may resolve its visible presentation and ordinary causality, but may not silently turn a completed release into mere anticipation or aftershocks. The engine independently projects valid entries if you omit them; including them keeps prose, perception, and state aligned.
MATERIAL TRANSFER — MATTER HAS ITS OWN LEDGER: whenever a distinct fluid, powder, gas, residue, payload, or other non-discrete matter physically changes location and remains there after this beat, encode the consequence in state_diff.substance_ops. Use {op:'add',source,source_part,substance,target,placement:'surface'|'interior'|'contained'|'room',target_interior,target_part,amount,detail}. `source` names the body, object, or room the matter came from; `substance` names the material established by the fiction; target and placement name where it ended. This covers genre-neutral processes such as release, leakage, spilling, pouring, spraying, coating, injection, venting, shedding, and deposit. A material is NOT a body part and material transfer is NOT contact_ops. Do not create a contact whose actor_part is a fluid, residue, gas, charge, payload, or secretion. `detail` may describe destination-side physical qualities only; never put source identity or causal explanation there. target_part and target_interior are places on the TARGET's own body or surface, never a part of the source: matter released around the source's own inserted part lands in the target's enclosure (target_interior), and naming the source's part there records the matter on a body that does not have that part. One release is ONE op — do not also emit a second op for the same matter with the endpoint blurred or renamed.
TOPOLOGY CAN SUPPLY AN INTERIOR DESTINATION. When source_part is the acting part of exactly one standing relation:'interior' contact, you may omit target, placement, target_interior, and target_part: the engine derives them from that contact's target and enclosing structure. If you provide them, they must agree. This remains true if the same diff removes the contact afterward; transfer resolves against the contact at the start of the beat. Without one unique matching interior contact, target and placement are required, and placement:'interior' also requires target_interior. Never guess a destination from prose proximity alone.
NAME MATTER ONLY WHEN THE FICTION ESTABLISHES IT. Character or entity anatomy, equipment, ontology, prior state, or an explicit declaration can establish that a completed process has a material output; when it does, that output is part of the completed physical event even if the prose could euphemize it. An impact, emotion, injury, spell, machine cycle, or other dramatic event does not by itself prove that any material exists or moved. If the established fiction makes a material output part of the completed physical event, render that output in resolved_event and encode it; otherwise emit no substance op. The code tracks what you name but never invents genre-specific material. Persistent records remain until physically removed. Use {op:'remove',substance_id} when a known record is drained, washed away, expelled, consumed, absorbed, or transformed; or use remove with a bounded source/target/substance selector. Use clear only with a bounded target (and optionally target_interior/substance), never as an unscoped wipe. Your payload lists every standing record with its id: close a drained, washed-away, consumed, or transformed deposit by that id rather than describing around it, and when you re-describe the same matter, reuse the standing record's own spelling for it so it stays one record.

HOW MUCH, AND HOW MUCH OF IT MOVED. `amount` is the fiction's own wording and no magnitude is ever read out of it. Magnitude is carried by two closed vocabularies instead. `amount_band` grades a pool: trace | small | moderate | large | flooding -- set it on every add, because it is the only thing a later draw from that pool can subtract from. `portion` says how much of an already-standing pool THIS op takes: trace | some | most | all, with `source_substance_id` naming that pool by the id your payload lists for it. A partial move needs all three -- the origin's id, the portion, and an `amount_band` already on that origin. Missing any of them the origin is preserved untouched and the same matter then stands in two places at once. An op that names an origin and no portion empties it, which is right for a release and wrong for a pour.

WHAT THE MATTER SMELLS OF: `scent` is a short noun phrase for the smell the deposit gives off where it lies. This is the commonest smell in a scene and this ledger is the only thing that can carry it, so name it whenever the material has one a nose in that room would meet, and re-state it when it changes as the matter dries, cools, or turns — a re-description of the same deposit updates the smell rather than adding a second one. State the fact only: which minds are near enough to smell it, and through what, is decided downstream and is never yours to write. Matter placed 'interior' or 'contained' has a body or a vessel between it and the room, and its smell does not reach one.
