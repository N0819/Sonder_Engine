BEING CARRIED — CONTAINMENT: this channel records a subject's enclosing or carrying relationship, not a duplicate account of a portable-object transfer. Choose the owner from what this row changes:
- A portable object is picked up, handed over, set down, pocketed, attached, worn, or otherwise changes carrier: objects writes inventory_ops. That operation sets the new holder and ends obsolete carriage. Do not also set or clear this object's containment for the same transfer; a null clear can erase the new holder. Keep any independent contact changes and request inventory_ops in required_channels if objects is absent from assigned_hands.
- A BODY becomes carried, enters an enclosure, rides another body, or leaves that relationship: write containment for the body. Its new room or station, if needed after release, belongs to spatial.
- An independent enclosure or concealment changes without transferring a portable object: write containment for that relationship. For example, lifting an enclosing cover off a stationary object can end its concealment. Name the actual established enclosure; do not invent a holder because prose merely says "hidden".
These are alternatives for ownership of the SAME relationship, not instructions for both hands to write it. If another relationship also changes, encode or request that separate effect.

Shape: containment:{name:{in,mode}|null}. Set {subject:{in:<existing holder>,mode:<mode>}} when enclosure or carriage begins; set {subject:null} when that relationship ends and this channel owns the release. Modes held|carried|riding|mounted|worn mean carried in view; pocket|container|inside mean shut away. A shut-away subject is concealed from the surrounding room and cannot see it, though touch and permitted hearing remain. Use only these modes: other words are treated as shut away. Being in view does not bypass the observer's ordinary perception limits.

Contact and containment express different facts: touching a shoulder does not establish being carried on it, and being in a pocket does not identify which surfaces touch. Request contact_ops only when the row establishes an independent contact effect. A new worn garment also needs body/attire work; possession does not complete the wardrobe.

A CARRIED BODY DOES NOT HAVE ITS OWN POSITION. The engine follows its carrier, including nested chains. Do not try to move a carried body by writing its room while containment still holds. On an actual escape, release the owned containment and request the independent movement from spatial. Preserve the Director's resolved outcome and encode only the actions it supplies; do not invent an escape or let a still-contained body act as though it stood freely in the room.

Containment is released automatically when either body changes size. If the resolved row says the relationship still holds afterward, supply its desired containment again. Standing containment is starting context; evaluate releases and re-entry in row order, never against a later state.
