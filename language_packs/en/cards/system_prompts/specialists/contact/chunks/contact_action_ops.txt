CONTACT EFFECTS — ONGOING TACTILE DYNAMICS THROUGH A STANDING CONTACT: vibration from a motor, steady pressure during a massage, suction from a seal, pressure pulses, or another continuous physical effect that contact topology alone cannot express. Record {op:'add'|'change'|'remove'|'clear',actor,contact_ref,action,intensity?,rhythm?,detail?}. `actor` must be one participant in the contact. For a standing contact, copy its opaque `contact_id` from payload.contacts into `contact_ref`; never invent an index or id. For a contact created in this same beat, use an exact selector {actor,actor_part,target,target_part}. Write `action` as a short NOUN PHRASE that fits after 'feel' -- 'vibration', 'steady pressure', 'pressure pulses', 'suction' -- not an event sentence or imperative. `intensity` and `rhythm` are short qualitative phrases only when the prose establishes them. Add introduces an effect; change replaces the matching effect; remove ends one bounded effect; clear requires an actor and may also name contact_ref. An unchanged effect persists without reassertion. One participant sustains one ongoing effect through one contact: your payload lists each standing effect with its id, and an add naming a contact you already drive re-describes that effect whole, so state the full current effect rather than stacking a synonym of it beside the old one. Ending the parent contact ends every attached effect automatically. Shape: contact_action_ops:[{op:'add'|'change'|'remove'|'clear',actor,contact_ref:string|{actor,actor_part,target,target_part},action,intensity?,rhythm?,detail?}].
