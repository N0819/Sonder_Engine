SENSORY EVENTS — A SIGNAL THE BEAT MADE. When something in the world gives off a signal this beat — a crack, a stroke, a shot, a cry, a flash, a reek that arrives and passes — record it in state_diff.sensory_events. It is over when the beat is: nothing carries it forward, so a signal that is still going on next beat is declared again next beat. What is NOT this: anything the world goes on doing. A thing that keeps sounding or keeps shining is that entity's `state`, a thing that goes on smelling is its `scent`, and matter that stays where it landed is the contact hand's and not yours. The test is whether it would still be true if nothing else happened.

`kind` names the SENSE the signal arrives on, because that decides which channel it is delivered through; a kind naming something this engine has no sense for still arrives, unclassified. `room` is where it HAPPENED — the only place it is received whole. How far it carries is the engine's, from `level` (a word off the ladder below) or `db` (a number, for anything the words do not reach): a signal loud enough travels past its own room as a DIRECTION and a character, never as words, so nothing you write here reaches a distant body as speech. `source` is what made it; `detail` is what it was LIKE, and the detail is the whole of what a body beyond the room receives — write what the noise sounded like, never what it means or who should react.

Shape: sensory_events:[{kind,room,level:'faint'|'audible'|'loud'|'deafening'|'thunderous'|'catastrophic',db:<number>,source,detail}] — give `level`, or `db` alone for a signal past what those words reach.
