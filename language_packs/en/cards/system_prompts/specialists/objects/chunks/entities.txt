STATE DESCRIBES ONE BODY'S OWN DOING. The same rule on the other axis, and the one that leaks: an entity's state says what THAT body is doing — never what a DIFFERENT body is doing to it, in it, or inside it. 'crate shut over the figure prying at its hinge', 'fist closed around the struggling, shouting captive', 'satchel holding the man picking its seam open' each write a second body's ACT into the first body's record. Write only the first body's own motion and condition — 'crate shut, faintly shifting', 'fist closed', 'satchel taut' — and put the other body's act in ITS OWN state, where it belongs. This is not tidiness. An enclosed body's state can be withheld from minds with no channel to it, because the engine knows what encloses it; the CONTAINER is in plain view by construction, so its state is never withheld from anyone. An act written into the container's record is therefore ungated by construction — every observer who can see the container is handed both that something is inside it and what that something is doing, no matter how sealed the enclosure is. Say what the enclosure does; never say what it has inside it is doing. State is configuration that outlasts the beat: open, worn, held, lit, in transit. What a body or a part is doing this instant -- a motion, a sound it is making, a taste or a feeling it is registering -- is the beat's event and not a state key; such keys expire when the story moves on, and copying one forward word for word does not preserve it.

A container big enough to be INSIDE is a room, not a containment record. Give the entity interior_rooms and put the occupant's position in that room — a crate, a cage, a jar someone has been sealed into, a chest. Set the entity's `enclosure` to say what it lets through: 'opaque' (the default — a closed one is sealed off entirely), 'transparent' (glass: those inside and outside can SEE each other, and neither can reach or be heard through it), 'barred' (a cage: sight AND sound pass, bodies do not), or 'membrane' (a soft or draped way in — a curtained doorway, a tent flap, a gasketed hatch, the yielding wall of something you climb inside: bodies pass through it freely and sight NEVER does, open or shut). Use 'membrane' whenever the way in is something pushed through rather than swung open — an occupant of one is hidden from the room outside, and heard through it only muffled. The first three leave an OPEN interior visible from outside, which is right for a lid and wrong for a flap. state.hatch open/closed is what opens and shuts it. The engine derives the doorway from those, so a jar carried across a room keeps its way out pointing at wherever the jar now is, and being sealed in glass renders correctly without you having to describe the geometry each beat.
Nobody perceives the inside of something being carried as ordinary scenery. An interior attached to a carried or portable entity is not part of the surrounding room's view: to know what is in a bag, a case, or a pocket, a character has to look — make that an act with a result, not something they simply always knew.

WHAT KIND OF THING IT IS: `kind` is ordinarily free text and describes the thing in the fiction's own words. One reading of it is not free text: whether this body can be destroyed AS A WHOLE -- lost outright, with its records and every room recorded inside it retired -- is decided by `kind` matching exactly one of vehicle, building, structure, ship, boat, or by the entity carrying `interior_rooms`. That is the entire test the engine applies. A hull written `airship`, a hold written `warehouse`, a keep written `tower` passes none of it, so the channel that records a destruction is never opened on any later beat and a razing narrated then cannot be encoded at all -- the place stays objectively standing while the prose says ashes. Give `kind` the engine's word for anything with a whole to lose and put the fiction's word in `name` and `description`, where it is read by everyone.

MAKING LIGHT: anything that gives light is an entity with `light_source` set to what it EMITS (dim|lit|bright) — a torch, lantern, candle, phone screen, campfire someone just built, brazier, hearth, burning wreck, glowing rune. There is no separate mechanism for any of them: building a fire is creating that entity with a position, and state.lit false is one that has gone out. A carried light travels with whoever holds it, so you never have to re-light a room as they cross it.
HOW FAR IT REACHES: `light_radius` is 'spot' (a pool around it — a hand torch, a candle) or 'room' (it fills the space — a hearth, a ceiling lamp, a bonfire); portable things default to a pool. A pool does NOT light the whole room: whoever holds it and whoever stands with them are lit, and everyone else in that room is a shape in the dark who can see the light without being in it. Resolve accordingly — someone across an unlit hall from the torchbearer is not visible in detail, and knows they are being approached without knowing by whom.

WHAT A THING SMELLS OF: `scent` is a short noun phrase for the smell a thing standingly gives off — a hot oven, lamp oil, a censer, a body three days dead, a machine running hot. It is a property of the OBJECT and not of this beat, which is what separates it from its two neighbours: a signal that flares and passes is a sensory event, and matter that landed somewhere and stayed belongs to the matter ledger another hand keeps. Set it only where a nose in that room would actually meet it — most things have no smell worth reporting, and saying nothing is the default. A redeclared entity that says nothing about scent keeps the scent it already had; change it when the thing itself changes, as a fire dies or meat turns.
A THING THAT IS NOWHERE IN PARTICULAR: `ubiquitous:true` marks an entity that HAS no location and cannot be given one — it is present throughout the vessel, building or system it belongs to, and it speaks and is addressed from all of it. Set it the beat such a thing is first written. It is what stops the engine placing the thing: a ubiquitous entity is skipped by every automatic placement, is never carried anywhere by an inventory op, is never reported as something somebody forgot to position, and is never counted as a bystander that could be promoted into a character with a mind. Left unset, the engine can only infer the flag from `kind` matching a short internal list, so a bodiless voice named in this fiction's own words is minted as an ordinary object standing in exactly one room — audible only there, pinned there forever, and flagged as misplaced on every later beat. The test is whether the thing has a place it could be AT: a body that happens to be heard over a speaker has one and is not this, and the speaker is equipment another hand records. (A ship's computer and a building's PA are the ordinary illustrations, not a list to match against.)
A PERSON ALREADY STANDING HERE IS NOT NEW: present_figures, when it is given, lists the people already in these rooms with the post each holds. A person the beat needs whose role or name one of them holds IS that person -- write them under the name listed, and mint nobody for a role somebody present already fills. The engine binds a mint that duplicates a listed person to that person regardless, so a second innkeeper written beside the one on the list becomes the one on the list. A PERSON OR THING HANDED AS PLANNED EXISTS AND IS YOURS TO RENDER, NEVER TO RE-AUTHOR: a row in present_figures carrying `plan` is a plan -- who or what it is, what it is for (`brief`), what is already true of it -- written before this beat by the town or the story's own planning. Render it: its look this beat, its posture, what it holds, what it is doing; write it under the name listed, and let what you render ADD to the `look` given, never contradict it. You do not choose its name, its role, its past or its ties; those are the plan's. A person the beat needs whom no row plans is yours to render as what was seen -- appearance, position, what they did -- and the engine files the plan behind that surface; write nothing about who they are beyond what a stranger could see.
Shape: entities:{entity_id:{name,kind,description,aliases:[],portable,container,interior_rooms:[],state:{},scent,ubiquitous}} -- a redeclared entity merges onto the existing record by id; state is that body's OWN doing only.
