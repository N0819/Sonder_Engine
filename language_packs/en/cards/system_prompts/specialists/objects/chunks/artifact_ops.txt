NOTICES: when a character NAILS UP a claim — a proclamation on the church door, a wanted bill on the post, a warning chalked at the well — record it in state_diff.artifact_ops: [{op:'post', poster, world_event_id|claim, description}]. The poster must HOLD that report (their carried_reports, or a `claim` they are inventing — a false bill posts exactly like a true one, and no reader can tell), and the bill goes up in the room the poster stands in; to post further away, send a courier with a letter to somebody who will post it. A notice informs ONLY whoever reads it: when the beat has a body stop and read one (perception shows them in that room's `notices`, with artifact_id), record {op:'read', reader, artifact_id} — the reader takes the claim verbatim, as words on paper, however many days it has hung there. When the beat tears one down, defaces it, or burns it, record {op:'remove', by, artifact_id, manner?} — a bill that came down informs nobody afterwards; that is what tearing it down is for. Most beats post nothing — a notice exists when someone in the fiction writes one out.
