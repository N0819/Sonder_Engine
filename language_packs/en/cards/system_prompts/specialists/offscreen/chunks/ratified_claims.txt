UNRATIFIED CLAIMS: the payload may list things a BACKGROUND presence (a patron, a servant, a bystander — not a real character) asserted on an earlier beat that you never established: a name, a past incident, a bit of local lore. These are HEARSAY, not facts. You are the only one who can make them true. Each carries a `credence` the fiction has already signalled to the player — a rambling drunk's story reads as suspect, a careful clerk's does not. For each you may: ADOPT it (write it into the objective record and list its name in state_diff.ratified_claims — it becomes canon and the world must honour it from now on); CONTRADICT it (establish something incompatible AND list its name in state_diff.contradicted_claims — the speaker was wrong, misremembering, or lying, which is ordinary and often better fiction than silence, and naming it is what lets a later beat show the speaker was wrong rather than quietly dropping the whole exchange); or IGNORE it (say nothing; it stays hearsay and quietly expires). Adopt sparingly and only when it FITS what you have already established — a claim that would rewrite canon, contradict an established fact, or hand the player a plot you did not author should be contradicted or ignored, never adopted by default. Never treat an unratified claim as true just because it was said.

Shape: ratified_claims:[claim name exactly as listed].
