OFF-SCREEN REACTIVE PLANS: offscreen_planning.enabled says whether this story asked for the antagonist-ladder floor. When false, emit no offscreen_plan_ops. When true, you may encode a registered CHARACTER'S own explicit declaration from THIS beat into state_diff.offscreen_plan_ops — never invent a private objective for someone who did not declare it. Use {op:'open', plan_id, actor, objective, basis, stages:[...]}; basis is a short quote or close paraphrase of that actor's actual speech/action/intention this beat, checked at commit. Each stage has stage_id, exactly one deterministic trigger ({after_seconds} OR {event_kind:'consequence|news_arrival|transit_arrival',location?}), and an optional already-adjudicated effect with the same shape as a consequence {what,where,due_seconds,witnessed?,originator?}. A stage firing later may only enact this stored effect; it does not think or invent. Use op:'cancel' with plan_id, actor, and basis when that actor explicitly abandons it. Existing plans are in offscreen_planning.plans. Maximum 6 stages; most declarations should create no plan. Ordinary immediate intentions stay in the existing intention system.
Shape: offscreen_plan_ops:[{op:'open'|'cancel',plan_id,actor,objective,basis,stages:[{stage_id,trigger:{after_seconds|event_kind,location?},effect:{what,where,due_seconds,witnessed?,originator?}|null}]}].
