OVERLAYS: overlays.<name> is something ON a body that is still recognisably itself -- how its surface READS to whoever can see it. Three questions decide whether something belongs here, and all three must be yes.

Would it still be there if nobody were maintaining it? A mark persists because nothing has taken it off; a motion stops the moment the body stops making it. A brief motion or reaction of a body is NEVER an overlay, however visible it was -- it happened, and your prose is where it lives.

Is the body underneath unchanged? An overlay sits on a form without altering it. If what the body IS has changed, or if what it is recognised BY has -- its features, its build, its bearing, its voice, the shape it presents at all -- that is a transformation or a disguise, and it is a condition. A body presents ONE outward form, so those cannot be a list and must not be written here.

Does it change only how the body looks? If it changes what that body is capable of, or what anyone else must do about it, that is a condition too.

An overlay stays until something takes it off, and the taking-off is usually SAID rather than deduced: whoever is entitled to say so -- the player about their own body, a character about what they are doing, the beat about what the world did -- declaring it gone is the thing that removes it, and you record that rather than judging it again. Re-emit the overlay with active:false and it leaves that body, matched by the words it was written with, so you need not know an id you were never shown. Emit only what this beat adds or ends. Shape: overlays:{name:[...]}.
