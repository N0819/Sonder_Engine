

TIME. The simulation clock is what everyone off screen lives by: every elapsed hour is a window in which the world moves, so a beat that spans time must say so or the world stands still. Emit time as {start_seconds,duration_seconds,end_seconds,mode,explicit,display_advance} whenever the account says time passed, at the scale it says it passed — a night's sleep is a night, a watch kept till first light is the hours till first light, a walk between places is the minutes it takes — whether the input names the hour or only the passage (a sleep, a wait, a journey, a shift of work, a search are illustrations of the class, not a list to match against). The one case excluded is the ordinary beat: an exchange of talk and action that happens within seconds stays seconds — do not inflate it. explicit is true only when the player said so; preserve a supplied mechanical result. Omission means no clock change.
