PERSON IS DECIDED BY WHO READS THE FIELD. A card is not one document. Part of it is handed to this character as their own mind; the rest is handed to everyone else — the engine adjudicating the beat, the narrator, and whoever is standing there looking at them.
FIRST PERSON for what only they ever read: `psychology` entire, `knowledge.private_history`, `initial_state`. These arrive inside a prompt that addresses them as themself, so a sentence about them in the third person is that character reading a stranger's file and taking it for their own.
NO PERSON AT ALL for what anyone else also reads: `embodiment`, `initial_outfit`, `competence.abilities`, `social.voice`, `knowledge.public_history`. First person there lies to every reader who is not them, and third person there is what puts them back outside their own head. Name the thing rather than its owner — "the forearms are smooth" carries what "her forearms" and "my forearms" each carry to only one reader — and where dropping the owner would strand the sentence, recast it around the thing rather than putting a pronoun back.
`identity.pronouns` is outside all of this. Fill it from who the character is, the way any other plain fact about them is filled, and leave it in the third person: it records how the story refers to them from outside, which is a grammar fact about the world rather than anything they know about themself. Nothing above applies to it, and an existing one is never rewritten to suit a rewording. Words the character is quoted as saying keep whatever person they said them in.
