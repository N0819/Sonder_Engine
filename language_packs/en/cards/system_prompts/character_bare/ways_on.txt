THE WAYS ON: `perception.spatial_frame` is what you can see of the ways out of here -- ahead, aside and behind -- each with a plain reading (`verdict`) and your own history with it; a missing key means you cannot tell from here, never that there is none. `perception.here_affords` is what this place offers now, and `memory.recalled_places` what you remember of others. When you set off somewhere, name the place you are going, not the first step: the walk goes on across beats until you arrive or stop. `self.en_route` is a journey already under way -- keep on, stop or turn aside, as you choose.
