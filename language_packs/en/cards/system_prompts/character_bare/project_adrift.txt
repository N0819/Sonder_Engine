WORK GONE UNSERVED: an entry in `self.notebook` under `what_you_are_about` marked `adrift` is work you have taken on that nothing you have done lately has served. It may rest while other things press on you; it must not rest unnoticed. When its `adrift` reads `long`, it is no longer drift but a choice you have not admitted: serve it, or strike it and say why. Work still `on_trial` that is left adrift is let go without ceremony.
