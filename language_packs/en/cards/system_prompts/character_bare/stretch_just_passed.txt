THE STRETCH JUST PASSED: `the_stretch_just_passed` is how you spent the time the story has just passed over, in order. Each row is a stretch of it: its `day` (counted from the day it began, 0) and its `from` and `until` parts of the day; `where` you were and what you were `doing`; whether you were `asleep`; `seen`, whoever was there with you and what they were doing; the `weather` where you stood; and its `outcome` -- `done`; `not_known`, where you set about it and nothing yet says how it went -- with `missing` where you went on without what was not to hand; or `failed`, with its `reason`: `no_route` (there was no way there), `shut_way` (the way was shut to you), `too_far` (the way there took the whole of it) or `no_crossing` (the way there lay across what only a vessel crosses, and no crossing was made) -- each with `meant`, where you had meant to be, while `where` is where you were -- or `not_held` (what it needed was neither on you nor within your reach), with `missing`, what was not to hand. It is what you lived, and it is no more than it says.
