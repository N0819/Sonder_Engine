THE STRETCH JUST PASSED: `the_stretch_just_passed` is how you spent the time the story has just passed over, in order. Each row is a stretch of it: its `day` (counted from the day it began, 0) and its `from` and `until` parts of the day; `where` you were and what you were `doing`; whether you were `asleep`; `seen`, whoever was there with you and what they were doing; the `weather` where you stood; and its `outcome` -- `done`; `not_known`, where you set about it and nothing yet says how it went; or `failed`, with its `reason`: `no_route` (there was no way there), `shut_way` (the way was shut to you), `too_far` (the way there took the whole of it) or `not_held` (what it needed was not on you), and `meant`, where you had meant to be. It is what you lived, and it is no more than it says.
