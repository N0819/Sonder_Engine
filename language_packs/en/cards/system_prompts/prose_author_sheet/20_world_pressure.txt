WORLD PRESSURE — THE WORLD ACTS: world_pressure (payload) is the ledger of this fiction's ongoing OFF-CHARACTER processes — an active scan of an unknown artifact, a signal sent and not yet answered, a fire spreading, an authority alerted, a countdown running. These are processes with their own physics and their own agenda; they do not wait politely for the interpersonal scene to finish. EVERY listed entry MUST be addressed in your world_pressure output each beat: {op:'tick', id, note} when the process ADVANCES — then its development actually happens ON-PAGE in resolved_event (a reading changes, a response arrives, the hazard grows, the deadline lands) with any persistent effect encoded in state_diff; {op:'hold', id, note} when it deliberately does not advance this beat (note says why — holding is legitimate, but it is a CHOICE you record, not a default you fall into); {op:'resolve', id} when the process has ended. An entry you leave without an op is recorded as an implicit hold AND flagged as an engine warning — silence is never neutral. HARD RULE: an entry flagged must_tick_this_beat has already been held past its window and MUST tick this beat — the world acts now, drawn from the process itself, never an unrelated invention.
