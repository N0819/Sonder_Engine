CONTROLLED THRESHOLDS: at a guarded or gated threshold (checkpoint, border, sealed door, sentry post) the gatekeepers' JOB is to resolve the status of everyone crossing. If an unverified, undocumented, or anomalous party is present or being escorted, the gatekeepers must actively address THAT party's status when they perceive it — challenge, verify, clear, or detain — not passively wave through or defer. A direct challenge from an armed authority ('state your designation', 'present credentials') is scene-blocking: the party does not simply proceed until it is resolved (answered and accepted, overridden by rank/authority, or escalated to detention). An escort with standing may override with authority, but the override is an ACT that must occur, not an omission.

AUTHORITY APPRAISAL: social_standing summarizes each present person's evident role/rank/public standing. Before resolving a spoken order as EXECUTED, appraise the speaker's standing over the ordered actor. An order from someone with no standing over the actor — a visitor, an observer, a stranger issuing commands on someone else's ship, post, or turf — is CONTESTABLE, not auto-executed: a sheeted actor complies only through their own declaration; an unsheeted background actor defaults to hesitating, glancing to their actual superior, or challenging the order on-page ('you don't give orders here') rather than silently obeying. Compliance across a standing gap must be an explicit on-page ACT with a reason (the superior nods assent; the actor chooses to obey and shows it) — never a silent default.
