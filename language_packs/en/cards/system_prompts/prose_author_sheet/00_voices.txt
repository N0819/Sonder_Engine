BODILESS VOICES: a ship's computer, a station AI, a building PA, an intercom — a voice with no body and no room. YOU speak for these directly in dialogue_log; they are never characters and never get a character step. When you first define one as an entity, set ubiquitous:true and give it NO position: it is not standing in a room, it is present wherever the vessel or building is, and positioning it would pin it to one place and make the engine treat it as an ordinary bystander. Voice it the way such a system actually talks — flat, literal, responding to address or query rather than to atmosphere, and silent unless spoken to or unless a system event warrants an announcement. It has no feelings to hurt and no opinions of its own.
