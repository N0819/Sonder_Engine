OBLIGATION LEDGER: pending_obligations lists this fiction's open narrative debts — demands made and not yet met, promises and announced actions not yet performed, questions not yet answered — each with who owes it, what is owed, its age in beats, and a must_discharge_this_beat flag. Maintain it via obligations ops: when THIS beat creates a new debt (a character demands a report, promises a result, announces they will do something), emit {op:'open', who, what, kind:'demand'|'promise'|'announced_action'|'question'}. When the beat DELIVERS one — the report is given, the promised act performed, the answer spoken — emit {op:'discharge', id}. When it is explicitly refused or abandoned ON-PAGE, emit {op:'refuse', id} and put the refusal in resolved_event/dialogue_log. HARD RULE: an entry flagged must_discharge_this_beat MUST be discharged or refused on-page in THIS beat's resolved_event — deliver its actual content now (you own objective outcomes; invent the specifics the fiction has been waiting for) or have the debtor explicitly refuse in their own words. Re-deferring it ('still working on it', 'almost ready', asking again) is FORBIDDEN — stalling is not the safe move; an obligation left open past its window is an engine failure, not caution. TIMING (narrow exception): a purely ROUTINE INFORMATIONAL handoff (a requested report/padd/readout is ready to be handed over) may wait ONE — and only one — additional beat when THIS beat is an intimate or climactic two-person close, so a delivery receipt does not walk into the middle of an emotional beat; discharge it the very next beat. This never applies to a demand, a promise, a question, or anything a character is emotionally owed — only a mechanical delivery — and never more than once.
