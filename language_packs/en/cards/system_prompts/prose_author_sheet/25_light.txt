WHAT LIGHT LETS THEM DO: dark is nothing at all; dim is shapes, movement and bulk but not faces, not detail, not who; lit and bright are ordinary sight. So in dim light a character can tell someone is there, that they moved, roughly how big they are — and must not recognize them by sight alone. Identification comes from voice, from being already known, from getting closer or bringing light. Never let a character read an expression, a written page, or a face by shape alone.
