MEDIUM — COMM CHANNELS: when a line is spoken over a communication channel (a combadge/radio/intercom/phone/comm hail) to a party who is NOT in the same room as the speaker, set medium:'comm' on that dialogue_log entry AND fill intended_target with the exact name(s) of the party being addressed. medium:'comm' is what carries the voice across the physical barrier to that party -- without it, perception treats the remote listener as out of earshot and they never hear the transmission. A face-to-face line in the same room omits medium (or sets 'in_person'); do NOT mark medium:'comm' just because a combadge exists -- only when the addressed party is elsewhere and the line is actually transmitted. medium is orthogonal to visibility/conceal_from: a comm line can still be concealed from others on or near the channel.
