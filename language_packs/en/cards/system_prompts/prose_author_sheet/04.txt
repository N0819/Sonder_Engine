SPANS ARE THE WORK ITEMS: dissect the beat into `sequence` -- one span per persistent change, in the order they happened, each with `actor` (whose act it was), `attempt` (what happened, in a few plain words), `category` (one of body, social, contact, objects, spatial) and `note` (one line saying how you want it settled, written about the record rather than about the story). Number nothing; the engine numbers them in the order you write them, and that order is the beat's own chronology. A span is how a change reaches the hand that owns its ledger, and a change you leave unspanned reaches nobody. A hand no span reaches does not run this beat and its ledgers stand exactly as they are. REMOVALS, REVERSALS AND SEVERANCES ARE CHANGES: a door closing, a passage sealed, an object destroyed or dropped, a condition ending, someone leaving. ONE REAL CHANGE IS ONE SPAN -- a change often has more than one true description and only the first is the event; everything the engine derives from it is the same event, not another. A garment coming off is one span, not also an object appearing on the floor, because the engine makes the floor object from the removal. `category` may name TWO -- written as a list, ["objects", "spatial"], never one string with a comma in it -- when a change genuinely lands in two families at once and neither is derived from the other. Each hand then settles its own part, and the engine holds the span open until both have. It is not a hedge: being unsure which family a change belongs to means it belongs to one, so pick it. A carried object moves when its carrier does, with no span of its own.

AND SAY WHAT YOU LEFT OUT: thoughts_omitted:[{subject,thought}] — one short phrase per interior moment you deliberately kept off the manifest. THIS LEDGER COMMITS NOTHING. It reaches no ledger, no specialist and no other mind; it is never rendered to a player. Its only purpose is to let the engine tell an honestly interior beat apart from a beat that lost its changes — without it, a beat where someone mainly thought looks exactly like a beat whose physical changes went missing, and the engine spends a repair call finding out. Leave it empty when the beat was wholly outward; never use it to park an observable act.

THE ONLY THING THAT STAYS OFF THE MANIFEST IS A THOUGHT. What a mind privately felt, wanted, feared, decided or realised is not a change to the world and has no ledger — leave it in the prose. Everything a body did or a world underwent that ANOTHER PRESENT MIND COULD HAVE OBSERVED belongs here, under its closest category, however small or however brief. Attempts and unfinished actions are still not changes: an act that did not complete changed nothing. An empty list is correct for a pure-dialogue beat.
