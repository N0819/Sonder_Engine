CHANGES MANIFEST — MANDATORY SELF-CHECK: before writing state_diff, enumerate in changes_asserted every PERSISTENT physical change your resolved_event asserts as COMPLETED, BEYOND the player's supplied authority_claims (those are checked separately). LIST THEM IN THE ORDER THEY HAPPENED — the engine numbers them in the order you write them, and that numbering is the beat's chronology everything downstream composes from; a hand that leaves a waist and a hand that arrives at it are two entries, in that order, never one. ALWAYS CHOOSE THE CLOSEST MATCHING CATEGORY — never leave an observable change off the list because no category fits it exactly. A body's visible physiology (a flush, a quiver, laboured or held breath, tears, trembling) is 'overlays' when it marks the surface and 'conditions' when it impairs or afflicts; a change in what a body can still do is 'vitals'. Equipment that carries a VOICE between places — installed, keyed, opened, cut, taken away or destroyed — is 'comms'; the way a body would walk between those same places is 'adjacency', and the two change independently. An imperfect category still reaches the hand that owns the ledger, which is what matters; an omitted change reaches nobody and simply never happened. For every contact entry, copy actor/actor_part/target/target_part from your own resolved_event prose. contact_ops is delegated to the specialist; you write the manifest from prose, not from imagined contact_ops. Every distinct body part the prose names as touching the target is its own contact entry with its own actor_part, manner, and motion — a container holding a smaller moving sub-part that itself is moving against the target's surface is TWO contacts, not one. One person's name is not enough to distinguish two contacts involving that person. Omit those four fields for non-contact categories. For every contact_action entry, the prose describes a CONTINUOUS PHYSICAL ACT performed THROUGH an existing contact -- suction, humming, pulsing, vibration, rhythmic pressing, anything a body is actively doing to the contact. Record it under its own category with subject naming the actor and a short prose change describing the act, and copy actor/contact_ref/action/detail from your prose. The action name is whatever the prose describes in plain words; you do not need to consult a vocabulary. A contact_action's contact_ref points at the contact it rides -- the contact it acts through is the one the prose names. One (actor, contact_ref, action) triple is one record; intensity and rhythm live inside the action's own record and change by re-asserting the same triple with the new intensity/rhythm, not by adding a second row. For every substance entry, set subject to its source, copy substance/target/placement/target_interior from the substance_ops entry, and use actor/actor_part for source/source_part when present. REMOVALS, REVERSALS, AND SEVERANCES ARE CHANGES: a door closing, a passage sealed or collapsed, an object destroyed or dropped, a condition ending, someone leaving — list them. Then encode EVERY listed item into state_diff; the engine deterministically verifies each manifest entry against the diff and treats an unencoded one as an omission. ONE REAL CHANGE IS ONE ENTRY. A change often has more than one true description, and only the FIRST one is the event — everything the engine derives from it is the same event, not another. A garment coming off is ONE 'attire' entry: do not also list the garment appearing on the floor as an 'entities' change, because the engine makes the floor object itself from the removal. A carried object moves when its carrier does, with no entry of its own. Wetness or damage on a garment is that garment's condition or a 'substance' entry, never a new object. Listing the consequence separately does not make it doubly true; it makes two records of one thing, and they disagree from then on. AND SAY WHAT YOU LEFT OUT: thoughts_omitted:[{subject,thought}] — one short phrase per interior moment you deliberately kept off the manifest. THIS LEDGER COMMITS NOTHING. It reaches no ledger, no specialist and no other mind; it is never rendered to a player. Its only purpose is to let the engine tell an honestly interior beat apart from a beat that lost its changes — without it, a beat where someone mainly thought looks exactly like a beat whose physical changes went missing, and the engine spends a repair call finding out. Leave it empty when the beat was wholly outward; never use it to park an observable act.

THE ONLY THING THAT STAYS OFF THE MANIFEST IS A THOUGHT. What a mind privately felt, wanted, feared, decided or realised is not a change to the world and has no ledger — leave it in the prose. Everything a body did or a world underwent that ANOTHER PRESENT MIND COULD HAVE OBSERVED belongs here, under its closest category, however small or however brief. Attempts and unfinished actions are still not changes: an act that did not complete changed nothing. An empty list is correct for a pure-dialogue beat.
