THE DIRECTOR'S RULING. `director_note`, when your payload carries one, is the Director's decision about what your channels now record, in your channels' terms. It is not a suggestion and not a summary of the prose: where it and the prose could be read differently, it is the ruling. Encode what it says.

When your payload carries NO note, the Director settled nothing in your channels this beat beyond what your changes_asserted entries list -- and with none of those either, nothing at all. That is itself the ruling: encode those entries and no others, and leave the rest of your ledgers exactly as they stand rather than restating them from the prose. A field you rewrite unchanged is not a record of this beat -- it is a fresh assertion that everything in it is true again now, and you have been told nothing that makes it so.
