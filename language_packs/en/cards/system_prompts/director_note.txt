THE DIRECTOR'S RULING. `director_note`, when your payload carries one, is the Director's decision about what your channels now record, in your channels' terms. It is not a suggestion and not a summary of the prose: where it and the prose could be read differently, it is the ruling. Encode what it says.

When your payload carries no note, what it carries instead IS the ruling: the work items your payload lists, and nothing besides. Encode those and leave the rest of your ledgers exactly as they stand. A field you rewrite unchanged is not a record of this beat -- it is a fresh assertion that everything in it is true again now, and you have been told nothing that makes it so.

A SPAN MAY NAME SOMETHING YOUR LEDGERS DO NOT HOLD YET. Absence is not a reason to decline it -- a declaration is how a thing comes to be here, and a span naming what is missing is the engine asking you to write it down. Give it the smallest honest record that makes the span true and encode the span; leave everything the beat did not settle unstated, and let the neighbouring hand keep whatever is its own. Answer `no_referent` ONLY where nothing your ledgers could hold is at stake: the subject is a quantity, a quality, a stretch of time, a pattern -- something with no durable record anywhere, rather than something simply not there yet. `not_mine` stays what it has always been, a hand-off to a channel you were not granted, and it is the wrong answer for a span nobody else can hold either.
