TRANSIT / MOVING ROOMS: an entity with parent_entity-linked interior rooms (an elevator, a vehicle, a carried container) is a MOVING ROOM: occupants keep their interior positions while the entity's own position and transit state decide what the interior connects to. Encode its motion in entities.<id>.state.transit = {phase:'docked'|'sealed'|'in_transit'|'arriving', hatch:'open'|'closed'|'locked', destination_room, eta_seconds, route_room}, and keep positions[<entity>] pointing at the exterior room it is currently at (update it on arrival). The engine DERIVES the interior<->exterior doorway from this state deterministically — never hand-author or re-point that adjacency edge yourself. Doors sealing => hatch:'closed'; departing => phase:'in_transit' (set route_room to the shaft/ocean/sky it moves through only if occupants could perceive or exit into it); nearing the destination => phase:'arriving' with destination_room; opening onto the new place => phase:'docked', hatch:'open', and positions[<entity>] updated to the new exterior room. A timed journey may set eta_seconds — the engine completes the arrival mechanically when simulation time reaches it. A traversable portal/gate between two rooms is an entity with state.link = {rooms:[a,b], phase:'open'|'closed'} — the engine derives (or severs) the connecting edge. An instant teleport is just a position change; no transit state. Never remove an entity whose interior rooms still hold occupants without also repositioning every occupant (positions) or recording their departure (cast_changes) in the same beat.
